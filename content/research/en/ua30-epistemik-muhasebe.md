# UA-30 — Epistemic Accounting: Theories of Consciousness and the Field's Own Errors

This dossier audits the consciousness sciences' own books: when several theories fit the same data and the choice among them is underdetermined, how does the field correct its own errors — and is that capacity a kind of **epistemic immunity**? The answer has two layers: where method is held above narrative, immunity is **real and measured**; where it would require changing a theorist's belief, it is approximately **nil**. That distinction is then applied to this program itself.

## 1. Intersection Map: Philosophy of Science, Theories of Consciousness and Sociology of Science Keep the Same Books

Four lines intersect here:

1. **Philosophy of science** — falsifiability, underdetermination, demarcation disputes.
2. **Theories of consciousness** — GNWT, IIT, higher-order theories, attention schema, predictive processing, illusionism, panpsychism.
3. **Methodology and metascience** — preregistration, registered reports, p-curve, multi-lab replications, adversarial collaboration.
4. **Sociology of science** — schools, incentive structures, generational turnover, the "nobody changes their mind" phenomenon.

Why do they not merge? Because merging them yields a conclusion that unsettles both sides: **the field's self-correction comes not from the honesty of individuals but from institutional design (keeping the record before the data).** This simultaneously rejects the romanticism of "scientists are honest anyway" and the cynicism of "science is just a belief system."

## 2. Anomaly Inventory (with citations)

### 2.1 Theoretical plurality and underdetermination

The same experimental data set can be explained by at least five major theory families: GNWT (Dehaene & Changeux, 2011), IIT (Tononi et al.; version 4.0 in Albantakis et al., 2023, *PLOS Computational Biology* 19:e1011465), higher-order theories (Lau & Rosenthal, 2011), attention schema theory (Graziano & Webb, 2015), and predictive processing (classified in families in the review by Seth & Bayne, 2022, *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* 23:439–454). This is Popper's "many theories, few discriminating predictions" problem in its consciousness-science form.

The critical point: **the proliferation of theories comes not from a scarcity of data but from an excess of degrees of freedom.** IIT's mathematical surface offers a manifold wide enough that, after any failed prediction, another prediction can be derived (the fact that after the COGITATE results both defenders and critics can simultaneously say "the theory was not falsified" is proof of this).

### 2.2 COGITATE: the first big test of adversarial collaboration

Researchers around Lau, Dehaene, Tononi and Koch pre-published an **adversarial collaboration protocol** in 2023 (Melloni et al., *PLOS ONE* 18(2):e0268577): the falsifiable predictions of the two theories would be tested by a theory-neutral consortium, with both sides' approval.

The results appeared in *Nature* in 2025 (Cogitate Consortium, *Nature* 642:133–142): 256 participants, fMRI + MEG + invasive EEG, multi-site, preregistered. The findings:
- **IIT's core prediction failed:** the sustained synchronization expected in the posterior cortex during conscious perception was not found.
- **GNWT's core prediction partially failed:** "ignition" was observed at stimulus onset, but the expected ignition at stimulus offset was largely absent, and the prefrontal representation was weaker than the theory predicted.
- **Neither theory was refuted, both were weakened** — and perhaps most importantly: **no proponent changed their mind.**

That last point is the most honest line in epistemic accounting: self-correction operates not one-to-one with data but **at the level of the literature and across generations.**

### 2.3 The demarcation dispute: is IIT "pseudoscience"?

In 2023, an open letter (a PsyArXiv preprint, "The Integrated Information Theory of Consciousness as Pseudoscience") signed by 124 signatories, including Fleming, argued that IIT — especially its panpsychist implications — cannot count as science. The counter-view: a theory that enters an adversarial collaboration and **states its own predictions in a falsifiable form in advance** stands, by definition, on the science side of the line.

The dossier's stance: **this dispute is a falsifiability dispute, not a truth dispute.** IIT's fate will be decided not by a pseudoscience label but by HZ-30's formula: the number of independent replications of its preregistered predictions.

### 2.4 The theoretical attack: "the unfolding argument"

Doerig, Schurger, Hess and Herzog (2019, *Consciousness and Cognition* 72:49–59) argued that causal-structure theories (including IIT) cannot explain consciousness: a system's functional behaviour can equally be realized by a feedforward system; if it performs the same function, then the theory's "structure" criterion must either count both as conscious or move its criterion to a non-functional place. This is a **non-experimental** critique, and it points in the same direction as COGITATE's "no posterior synchronization" result: the theory's measurable signature is becoming inconsistent with its own core.

### 2.5 The field's own error chain (brief but instructive)

The same signature appears outside consciousness science; a few **catalogued** cases:

1. **The prefrontal "NCC" error.** For a period the prefrontal cortex was believed to be where consciousness lives; controlled designs showed this was confounded with **attention, report and introspection** (Frässle et al., 2014, *Journal of Neuroscience* 34:1738; Boly et al., 2017, *Journal of Neuroscience* 37:9603–9613).
2. **The Libet reading.** Against the reading "the brain decides before we do", it was shown that the readiness potential may be not a decision but **an accumulation of random fluctuations** (Schurger, Sitt & Dehaene, 2012, *PNAS* 109:E2904–E2912).
3. **Ego depletion.** While the aggregate effect of around 200 studies was moderate, a preregistered replication across 23 laboratories reduced the effect **practically to zero** (Hagger et al., 2016, *Perspectives on Psychological Science* 11:546–573).
4. **Power posing.** Hormonal and behavioural effects were not found in replication (Ranehill et al., 2015, *Psychological Science* 26:653–664); a p-curve analysis showed the evidence base had been inflated by selective reporting (Simmons & Simonsohn, 2017, *Psychological Science* 28:687).
5. **The Stanford prison experiment.** An archival review showed the "power of roles" narrative was produced by the experiment's **instructions and demand characteristics** (Reicher & Haslam, 2006, *British Journal of Social Psychology* 45:1; Le Texier, 2019, *American Psychologist* 74:823).
6. **The marshmallow test.** When family background and cognitive-control covariates were added, the effect largely vanished (Watts, Duncan & Quan, 2018, *Psychological Science* 29:1159).

The shared signature: **the effect is strong in the first study and weak under controlled replication, while the narrative expands every time — "actually it holds under these conditions."**

### 2.6 The self-correction machine: what actually works?

- **Preregistration** and **registered reports** (Chambers, 2013, *Cortex* 49:609): the prediction is recorded before the data; the record cannot be altered afterwards.
- **P-curve and selective-reporting audits** (Simonsohn, Nelson & Simmons, 2014): the distribution of published p values yields the fingerprint of p-hacking.
- **Multi-lab replications** (Hagger 2016; Open Science Collaboration 2015, *Science* 349:aac4716: only 36% of 100 studies were significant again).
- **Adversarial collaboration**: parties co-write mutually falsifiable predictions (COGITATE).
- **Preprints/peer review and data sharing** against the structural flaws that Ioannidis (2005, *PLoS Medicine* 2:e124) identified in "most published research findings are false."

The effect of these instruments is **measured**: effect sizes in preregistered replications come out systematically smaller than in the originals. Self-correction is thus not a romantic slogan but a statistical signature.

### 2.7 Where self-correction does not work

The same trick does not always work:
- **At the theorist level:** after COGITATE, proponents did not abandon their theories; objections shifted to methodological detail.
- **At the demarcation level:** the "pseudoscience?" dispute proceeds by definitions, not by data.
- **At the hard-problem level:** the distinction between Chalmers's (1995) formulation and the illusionism of Dennett/Frankish (Frankish, 2016, *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 23:11–39) **does not close with data**; even Chalmers's "meta-problem" proposal (2018) does not solve the problem, it relocates it.

In short: self-correction is strong for **empirical claims** and weak for **conceptual frameworks**.

## 3. Three-Level Excavation

### Level 1 — Observation

What is observed: (a) the grand theories of consciousness do not determine the data — the same data fit several theories; (b) adversarial collaboration is possible, and when it happens the theories' preregistered predictions can be tested; (c) the result weakens the theories but does not persuade their proponents; (d) by contrast, the field's **published evidence base** really does correct itself: errors like ego depletion, power posing and the prefrontal NCC are cleared out of the literature; (e) the correction is a property of the record, not of the individual.

### Level 2 — Pattern

The pattern is: **error arises from fitting data to narrative; correction comes from keeping the record before the data.** Preregistration is the technical name of the immunity: when the prediction is written down before the outcome is seen, narrative flexibility disappears — because the only source of flexibility is **retrospective adaptation**.

This is the epistemological twin of the structure found in HZ-40: there the observer's function was the **irreversible record**; here the researcher's function is the **timely record**. Both turn on **when** the record is made. In physics the record becomes irreversible through entanglement with the environment; in epistemology the record becomes irreversible against narrative through preregistration.

Second pattern: **protection is institutional, not individual.** COGITATE's success came not from "honest scientists" but from the parties having to have **their predictions approved by the other side**. Immunity comes not from the actors' virtue but from a design that swaps roles.

### Level 3 — Why is it like this?

Why do conceptual frameworks fail to close with data while empirical claims close?

Because an empirical claim can be reduced to **a single number** or a single falsifiable prediction, whereas a framework is a **language**, and languages are not falsified. "What is consciousness?" is not a measurement but a **choice of conceptual map**; maps "fit" the data as long as they are internally coherent at their own scale. This is why the hard problem does not get solved: because it requires not a solution but **a change of language**.

The deeper pattern beneath this (the third layer): **Science changes conceptual frameworks not through data but through generational replacement; it corrects data within itself.** Epistemic immunity is therefore a two-speed system: a fast channel (preregistration, replication, adversarial collaboration) corrects empirical claims; a slow channel (generational change) replaces frameworks. A program's credibility is its capacity to **distinguish which channel applies to which claim**.

And from this follows the harshest pattern turned on the program itself: **a program like Horizon Excavation is inclined to speak the language of the slow channel (pattern, candidate law, hidden pattern).** Its connection to the fast channel is possible only because of the falsifiable test lists and falsification boxes at the end of every dossier. This is why this dossier reads the program's own design as a matter of **epistemic security** (MP-4).

## 4. HIDDEN PATTERN CANDIDATE: HZ-30 — Epistemic Immunity

> **Candidate Law (HZ-30).** A claim's immunity to evidence is directly proportional to its number of preregistered predictions, independent replications and blinded designs, and inversely proportional to its narrative flexibility. When the record is kept **before** the data, flexibility disappears; this is the epistemological equivalent of physics' irreversible-record principle.

Simplified expression:

**EI = (P_preregistered × R_independent × K_blinded) / E_narrative**

where EI is epistemic immunity, P the number of preregistered falsifiable predictions, R the number of independent replications, K the blinded-design coefficient, and E the number of **distinct** retrospective adaptations that can be produced after a failed prediction.

**Three predictions:**
1. **Record-first effect:** for the same data set, effect sizes reported when the analysis plan was recorded before the data must be **systematically smaller** than those from post hoc analyses (the statistical signature of the replication crisis). This prediction is already partly confirmed.
2. **Flexibility–falsifiability scale:** theories with high E (those able to generate many retrospective adaptations) should more often "partially weaken" on preregistered tests but **less often be refuted**; low-E models either fail fast or confirm fast. Prediction: a theory's historical survival time correlates positively with its E — a measure not of its truth but of its flexibility.
3. **Institutional superiority:** adversarial collaboration raises the clarity of claims in the literature even when it does not change the parties' beliefs. Prediction: after adversarial collaboration, the **number of a theory's claims decreases while their specificity increases** (a measurable text analysis).

**Discriminating test (HZ-30/1 — blinded adversarial triage).** Three claims from Horizon Excavation itself are selected (e.g. the tests HZ-35/1, HZ-36/1, HZ-40/1); each is converted into a **preregistered, blinded** protocol and handed to independent laboratories with the theory labels removed. Prediction: claims with high EI scores replicate in the predicted direction; low-scoring ones reverse direction in at least one independent attempt. This test measures the program's own immunity — the one experiment it runs on itself.

**Second test (HZ-30/2 — E measurement protocol).** A theory's E value is measured by the number of "retrospective adaptation" defences published after a given failed preregistered prediction. Method: code whether the theory's **measurable signature definition** changed between pre- and post-result publications (two independent coders, high agreement required). Prediction: in post-COGITATE publications of IIT and GNWT, the signature definition has measurably **widened** relative to before the result.

**Third test (HZ-30/3 — red-team audit).** For every dossier in Horizon Excavation an **adversarial researcher** is assigned; their task is to take that dossier's falsifiable test list and write a **refutation plan** (which result would bring the candidate law down). Prediction: the audit will narrow the terms of the program's claims and reduce the number of HZs in the Φ-2 synthesis. If it fails (if no claim falls), that is evidence of a high E value for the program itself — and this dossier **records that against itself**.

## 5. Evidence Thermometer: 🟢 (self-correction at the method level) / 🟡 (grand theories of consciousness underdetermined)

**Why 🟢?** Self-correction's existence is not an opinion but a measurement: preregistered replications systematically shrinking effect sizes, p-curve analyses catching selective reporting, claims like ego depletion and power posing being cleared from the literature, adversarial collaborations like COGITATE being possible. At the method level, immunity is **demonstrated**.

**Why 🟡?** (1) The grand theories of consciousness are still not determined by data; COGITATE weakened two theories but did not elevate a third. (2) Self-correction does not change theorists' beliefs; correction operates on a generational scale. (3) For conceptual problems such as the hard problem, no correction mechanism exists.

> **What remains if it is refuted?** Even if HZ-30's claim that "institutional design outranks individual virtue" were to fall, the **record-first effect** (preregistered replications yielding smaller effects) survives — it is a fact demonstrated by independent meta-analyses, independent of this program. In the other direction: if preregistration, replication and adversarial collaboration produced **no systematic effect at all**, then the conclusion would be that the evidence base of consciousness science does not correct itself and that this is fate rather than design — in which case the program's own test lists would be decoration too, and the dossier accepts that.

## 6. Practical Implications and the Gateway Bridge

1. **A five-question reading protocol.** For any claim about consciousness or health, ask: (a) was the prediction recorded **before** the outcome? (b) was the analysis blinded? (c) is there independent replication? (d) did a holder of the opposing view approve the design? (e) how was failure defined? A claim that does not get five "yes" answers goes into the box marked "interesting but not immune."
2. **This protocol applies to Gateway claims too.** Preregistered, blinded designs are possible for the psychophysiological effects of Hemi-Sync applications (HZ-17) and for sleep/focus benefits — and this dossier opposes calling them "proven" without them. By the same token, the opposite claim, "it is only placebo," must pass the same protocol.
3. **This program is itself a claim-generator.** Horizon Excavation hunts for patterns across 40 areas; every pattern-hunting program is exposed to apophenia (seeing order in chance). The program's antidote is built into its design: an evidence thermometer, a falsification box and preregistrable test lists in every dossier. This dossier is the rationale for why that design is compulsory.
4. **Implication for practice.** In personal practice, separate the question "does it work?" from "is the theory true?" A technique may work despite being explained by a false theory (exercise, sleep hygiene); and it may fail despite being explained by a true theory. The everyday form of the record-first principle is: **before you try it, write down what will happen.**
5. **Research note:** This dossier is the twin law of HZ-40 (irreversible record in physics, timely record in epistemology) and establishes the methodological backbone of the Φ-2 synthesis; it is directly linked to MP-4 (epistemic security firewall) and Φ-1 (six-layer pattern).
