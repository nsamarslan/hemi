# Focus levels and the seven resonant colors — Monroe's map of consciousness

## What are the Focus levels?

Monroe mapped the deep states of consciousness that yogis and masters have described for centuries as **Focus levels** — trainable, repeatable states. Each level has a characteristic quality of consciousness; the transitions between levels are slow and can be learned.

## The basic ladder

[[storyboard:focusmap]]

- **Focus 3 — The Whole-Brain State.** Both hemispheres working at the same amplitude and frequency, in synchrony. Not a session in itself; the doorway to all levels.
- **Focus 10 — Mind Awake, Body Asleep.** While the body sinks into deep sleep, the mind stays awake and alert. The foundation of all practice.
- **Focus 11 — The Access Channel.** The passage between 10 and 12. The level where Human Plus function commands are encoded; in Miranon's description, here you can program your body and mind with whatever abilities you want.
- **Focus 12 — Expanded Awareness.** Awareness expands beyond the limits of the body; intuition strengthens, inner imagery vividifies, perception beyond the body is felt for the first time. The most-used second state of the series.
- **Focus 15 — No Time.** The flow of time dissolves; past, present and future coexist. The state of pure being.
- **Focus 18 — The Heart Level.** The vibration of unconditional love; corresponds to the rose color (the healing layer in the Miranon system).
- **Focus 21 — The Bridge.** The bridge to other energy systems, beyond physical reality. For many years worked only in residential programs; entered the home series with Odyssey in 2004.
- **Focus 22 — Here and There.** A state of contact with people who are physically alive but unconscious (coma, substance-induced states).
- **Focus 23 — The Transition Area.** The area inhabited by those who have just completed a physical life (entered the home series with Voyager).
- **Focus 25 — Belief System Territories.** The territories where beliefs are experienced as structures of their own.
- **Focus 27 — The Reception Center.** The resting and reviewing center between a physical life and the next (Voyager and Union).
- **Focus 34/35 — The Gathering.** The vast plane described in Monroe's *Far Journeys*; worked in advanced residential programs.
- **Focus 42 and 49.** Advanced levels belonging to the Institute's residential curriculum; also named in the CIA report.

## The seven resonant colors (Miranon)

[[storyboard:colors]]

Miranon is a being that appeared in the Institute's **Explorer** research sessions of the 1970s and conveyed information about the Focus levels (through an explorer named Leona with Shay Ellsworth, later drawn by Nancy McMoneagle; compiled by Daniel Erickson in 2023). According to Miranon, consciousness is a **repeating cycle of seven colors**:

- **Blue — Existence / awareness.** The moment you first notice an event; the introduction of a new concept.
- **Red — Awareness of self / reflection.** The mirror of blue: interpreting the event from your own standpoint; ego, personality, inner dialogue.
- **Yellow — Repair / reconciliation.** The reunion of blue and red; making the lesson a normal part of life; balance in mind and body.
- **Rose — Guidance and healing.** The vertical axis; the heart; unconditional love; instantly accessible by saying "thank you".
- **Green — Application of abilities in the current state.** The lesson settling into the body-mind condition.
- **Purple — Application of abilities in higher states.** Carrying the lesson into wider awarenesses.
- **White — Completeness.** The wholeness of the cycle; passage to higher existence.

Geometry: blue and red are horizontal (mirrors), yellow is their union, rose is vertical (the guidance/healing axis), white is the all-encompassing circle.

## The color–level cycle

The colors repeat every seven levels. By this cycle:

- Focus 10 — **Yellow** (balanced, unified physical consciousness)
- Focus 12 — **Green** (expanded awareness)
- Focus 15 — **Blue** (timeless existence)
- Focus 18 — **Rose** (the heart level)
- Focus 21 — **White** (the bridge; overlaps with the "white light" reported in NDEs)
- Focus 23 — **Red** (reflection in the transition area)
- Focus 25 — **Rose** (healing in the belief territories)
- Focus 27 — **Purple** (higher application in the reception center)

Miranon notes: levels are not rigid blocks but overlapping layers; the same experience can carry more than one color.

## How to use it

- At the start of every session, see its level (the Focus badges on the detail screen).
- In the entry phases, feel the transition from one level to the next; the transitions glide slowly over the whole entry.
