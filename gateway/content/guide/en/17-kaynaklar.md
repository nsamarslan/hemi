# Sources and further reading

This guide is a synthesis of hundreds of publicly available documents. Below is a list of sources grouped by type, noting what each contributed to the guide. No original recording or copyrighted text has been copied into this app; quotations are short and attributed.

## A. Primary documents (Monroe / the Institute)

- **The Gateway Experience Guidance Manual** (Monroe Institute; various editions, c. 1980s–2000s; PDF on archive.org, thelonerider.com and document-sharing sites). The Wave I–VI exercise directory, the tools (REBAL, ECB, EBT, Body Map, Color Breathing, Energy Walks, Grounding, One-Breath, Free Flow), the Prospecting descriptions, the Gateway Affirmation, the "Brain Power" section, Monroe's introductory comments. — The backbone of topics 5, 6, 7 and 8.
- **Gateway Intermediate Workbook / First-Stage Separation notes** (the Institute; in community archives). The five separation methods and the screen methods. — Topic 8.
- **The Human Plus booklet (1989)** (scanned text on archive.org). Function list, commands, rules of use, the Access Channel. — Topic 11.
- **Robert A. Monroe — Journeys Out of the Body (1971), Far Journeys (1985), Ultimate Journey (1994).** Locales I–III, Conditions A–D, the vibrational state, Miranon, Focus 27 and the "Park", retrieval, the I-There. — Topics 2, 4, 8.
- **Hemi-Sync / Monroe Institute product pages** (hemi-sync.com, monroeinstitute.org): the contents and release years of Waves I–VIII, the Lucid Dreaming series (1999), Chakra Attunement, the 5th Dimensional Transformation collection, the Support for Journeys Out of the Body series, Gateway Voyage Online (23 sessions), descriptions and prerequisites of the residential programmes. — Topic 10.
- **Miranon — "The Seven Resonant Colors"** (Monroe Institute archive; channelled via Leona/Shay Ellsworth, drawings by Nancy McMoneagle, 2023 archive note). The colour–focus mapping. — Topic 4.

## B. Official and academic documents

- **Wayne M. McDonnell — Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process (1983; CIA Reading Room, partially released in 2003, the missing page 25 added in 2021).** An attempt to explain Gateway through hologram/resonance theory; a summary of FFR, Hemi-Sync and the focus levels. — Topics 2 and 3; this guide does not present the report's theoretical speculation as evidence.
- **Coordinate Remote Viewing Stages I–VI and Beyond (1985; DIA/SRI, CIA Reading Room).** The CRV stages, AOL, aperture, the matrix. — Topic 9.
- **Ingo Swann — the CRV manual, and Paul H. Smith — Reading the Enemy's Mind (2005).** The history and method of CRV. — Topic 9.
- **Utts (1995) and Hyman (1995) — the AIR evaluation (Stargate).** Opposing conclusions. — Topic 9.
- **Robert Monroe — US Patent 3,884,218 (1975), "Method of inducing and maintaining various stages of sleep in the human being."** The patent basis of Hemi-Sync. — Topics 2 and 3.
- **Oster, G. (1973). "Auditory beats in the brain." Scientific American.** The classic paper on binaural-beat perception. — Topic 3.
- **Garcia-Argibay, Santed & Reales (2019). "Efficacy of binaural auditory beats in cognition, anxiety, and pain perception: a meta-analysis." Psychological Research.** Small-to-medium effect, high heterogeneity. — Topic 3.
- **Ingendoh, Posny & Heine (2023). "Binaural beats to entrain the brain? A systematic review…" PLOS ONE.** The inconsistency of the entrainment evidence. — Topic 3.
- **Atwater, F. H. (1997/2001). "Inducing altered states of consciousness with binaural beat technology" and "Binaural beats and the regulation of arousal levels."** Theoretical/technical summaries from the Institute. — Topic 3.
- **Jacobson, E. (1938). Progressive Relaxation; Kabat-Zinn, J. (1990). Full Catastrophe Living; LaBerge, S. (1985). Lucid Dreaming.** The source texts of the complementary practices. — Topic 12.

## C. Secondary sources and community

- **The Gateway Experience Fandom wiki** — an encyclopaedia of focus levels and exercises; a user compilation, used after checking against primary sources.
- **Reddit r/gatewaytapes, r/MonroeInstitute** — the Odyssey/Prospecting numbering confusion, the Voyager/Union releases, MSS remaster discussions, experience reports, troubleshooting practice (topic 14 drew on these).
- **Discogs / MusicBrainz** — wave orderings, cassette/CD catalogue numbers, durations.
- **The Monroe Institute blog and "Hemi-Sync FAQ"** — headphones, volume, safety, programme prerequisites.
- **Mindplace / binaural-maker forums** — cross-checks for beat-frequency approximations (the app's frequencies rest on such public descriptions; the Institute does not publish its signal design).

## E. How to read

1. First topics 1–5 of this guide; then Monroe's *Journeys Out of the Body*.
2. Read the (publicly available) manual PDF side by side with the app's Techniques tab; see the original description of each tool.
3. To keep the scientific balance: topic 3 + Garcia-Argibay (2019) + Ingendoh (2023).
4. For remote viewing: topic 9 + the CIA's 1985 CRV document.
5. For experience reports: the communities — but your journal is your primary source.
