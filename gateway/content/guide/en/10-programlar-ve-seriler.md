# All programmes and series — home series, album collections, residential programmes

Monroe's catalogue grew layer by layer over forty years. This topic gathers all of it on a single map: the home series (the ones in this app), the Hemi-Sync album collections and the Institute's residential/online programmes. The in-app counterpart is the Catalogue tab: **7 series, 17 waves, 145 sessions**.

## A. The series in the app (Catalogue)

### 1. Early Period · Journeys Out of the Body (1970s) — 6 sessions
Hemi-Sync "Support for Journeys Out of the Body": Introduction (in Monroe's voice), Relaxation Exercise, Conditions A, B, C, D. In the original everything after the second track is unguided; the app adds light reminders.

### 2. Waves of Change (1998–2022) — 51 sessions
- **Wave I Discovery (Focus 10):** Orientation · Intro Focus 10 · Advanced Focus 10 · Release and Recharge · Exploration Sleep · Free Flow 10.
- **Wave II Threshold (12):** Intro Focus 12 · Problem Solving · One Month Patterning · Color Breathing · Energy Bar Tool · Living Body Map.
- **Wave III Freedom (10/12):** Lift Off · Remote Viewing · Vectors · Five Questions · Energy Food · First Stage Separation.
- **Wave IV Adventure (12):** One Year Patterning · Five Messages · Free Flow 12 · NVC I · NVC II · Compoint.
- **Wave V Exploring (12→15, voiced 1998):** Advanced Focus 12 · Discovering Intuition · Exploring Intuition · Intro to Focus 15 · Mission 15 Creation and Manifestation · Exploring Focus 15.
- **Wave VI Odyssey (12→21, 2004):** Sensing Locale 1 · Expansion in Locale 1 · Point of Departure · Nonphysical Friends · Movement to Locale 2/Intro Focus 21 · Free Flow Journey in Focus 21.
- **Wave VII Voyager (23/25/27, 2019; based on Lifeline):** Explore Total Self · Intro Focus 23 · Intro Focus 25 · Intro Focus 27 · Retrieval · Messages From Beyond.
- **Wave VIII Union (27+, 2022; based on Exploration 27, 8 exercises):** Special Tour · Meeting the Entry Director · Educational Opportunities · Healing and Regeneration Center · The Planning Center · Coordination Area · Inner Earth · The Absolute. (+ the Free Flow 27 companion added by the app.)

### 3. Gateway · Original Series, Free Flow (1984–1996) — 12 sessions
Two fully instrumental waves: **Exploring** (Mission 10 · Mission 12 · Mission Day · Mission Night · Horizons · Pathways) and **Prospecting** (Metamorphosite · Bio-Body · Null Point · Plus Polarity · Near Reaches · Far Reaches). Voiced waves replaced them in 1998 and 2004; copies labelled "Wave VII Odyssey" among collectors are a trace of that renumbering.

### 4. Human Plus (H-Plus®) · Function Exercises (1987–1989) — 53 sessions
The 49 functions of the 1989 booklet + Zoning, Access To Energy, Do This Now, Hypertension. Command encoding through the Access Channel (Focus 11). Details: topic 11.

### 5. Gateway Voyage · Open Sessions — 2 sessions
The programme's Introductory Exercise and the 43-minute Open Exercise from the archive (1980). The rest of the programme consists of the Waves of Change I–VI sessions.

### 6. Hemi-Sync Series · Album Collections — 11 sessions
- **Lucid Dreaming (1999, Mark Certo):** Mind Awake/Body Asleep · Creative Imagination · Flying Solo (90 min each) · Programming Your Dream State (8 hours).
- **Chakra Attunement:** Chakra Meditation (Eluv; 51-min long + 23-min short) · Heal and Balance (Allyn Evans; the Institute's free daily chakra tune-up).
- **From the 5th Dimensional Transformation collection:** Morning Exercise (Monroe's voice, 19 min) · Releasing Early Morning Anxiety (20 min) · Transformation (Micah Sadigh, 49 min) · Exploring Our Future (Lee Stone, 58 min).

### 7. Extra Practices · Complementary Techniques — 10 sessions
Manual techniques (Energy Walk, Grounding, One-Breath) + non-Monroe practices proposed by this project (Body Scan, Box Breathing, Progressive Relaxation, Loving-Kindness, Dream Journal, WBTB, Evening Review). Details: topic 12.

## B. Hemi-Sync collections not in the app (for reference)

The Hemi-Sync catalogue contains dozens of titles; most are skill- or state-oriented rather than "journeys":
- **Mind Food®:** Concentration, Super Sleep, Sleep Deeply, Surf, Catnapper, Remembrance, Attention (H-Plus), Buy the Numbers…; **Wake-Induced Lucid Dreaming** and **Senses Initiated Lucid Dreaming** (Luigi Sciambarella).
- **Metamusic®:** music albums with Hemi-Sync — Octaves of Light, The Dreaming Gate, Beneath the Moon, Holomovement, Portraits, Touching Grace, Tribal Journeys, Where Earth Touches Stars, Transformation…
- **Album series:** Positive Immunity Program, Positively Ageless, Going Home (support for dying and transition; with contributions from Elisabeth Kübler-Ross and Charles Tart), Journey Home, The Shaman's Heart Program, Network of Light, Mediumship: The Training Ground (Suzanne Giesemann), Out-of-Body Techniques (William Buhlman), Chakra Journey (ThunderBeat), Heart-Sync, Exploring Other Lives, Timeless, Waves of Love, The Music of Graceful Passages.
- **Monroe Sound Science (MSS) remasters and the Expand app** (the Institute's own streaming app).

These titles are listed in the **Hemi-Sync reference catalogue** view at the bottom of the Catalogue tab (with categories and search). In PC source mode the app finds the matching folder or files in your library and plays them as they are; for albums the tracks continue in order. These recordings carry no spoken guidance from the app, the recording's own narration is used.

## C. Institute programmes — residential and online

The programmes are sequential; each takes the previous one as a prerequisite:

- **Gateway Voyage®** (6 days, the flagship; no prerequisite). Focus 10–21. Five to six exercises a day in CHEC units, group sharing, evening talks. The online form, **Gateway Voyage Online** (8–12 weeks, 23 sessions, Monroe's voice, MSS), also counts as a prerequisite.
- **Guidelines®** (prerequisite: Gateway). Deepens Focus 15 and 21; ongoing communication with the "Inner Self Helper"; out-of-body orientation exercises; work with a particular healing energy.
- **Lifeline®** (1991; prerequisite: Gateway). Focus 22–27; the emphasis on "service" — helping those who have not completed their transition (rescue/retrieval). The source of Wave VII Voyager.
- **Exploration 27 (X27)** (prerequisite: Lifeline or Guidelines). A week in the "Park" of Focus 27: the Entry Director, the Education Center, Healing and Regeneration, the Planning Center, the Coordinating Intelligences, the crystal at the earth's core. The source of Wave VIII Union.
- **Starlines I–II** (prerequisite: X27). The solar system and beyond; Focus 34/35 and above; contact with "stellar" consciousnesses.
- **Heartline** (prerequisite: Gateway). Compassion and heart-centred exploration (emphasis on Focus 18).
- **Timeline** (prerequisite: Gateway). The early years of this life, past lives, future perspectives.
- **OBE Spectrum**, **Lucid Dreaming Intensive**, **Energy Medicine Squared (EM²)**, **Conscious Presence**, **Portal Beyond Consciousness**, **Going Home** (2-day workshop), **Exploring Consciousness** (weekend), **Discovery** (weekend introduction), Gateway Voyage for Veterans and First Responders.
- **Online:** Gateway Voyage Online, Introduction to Beyond Meditation, Inner Guidance, Energy Expansion, Beyond Earth Life, Lifting the Veil, Lucid Dreaming Intensive (Virtual), Exploration 27/Lifeline (Virtual Retreat).

## D. Matching Institute programmes to the home series

- Gateway Voyage ≈ Waves I–VI (+ the Voyage open sessions). In the programme Focus 15/21 are done with a live trainer; in the home series Waves V/VI correspond to that.
- Lifeline ≈ Wave VII Voyager.
- Exploration 27 ≈ Wave VIII Union.
- Guidelines/Heartline/Starlines have no home equivalent; the Focus 15/18/21 free flows and the loving-kindness practice (Extra Practices) offer partial preparation.

## E. In what order?

1. **Beginner:** Wave I → II → III → IV (repeat each session until you feel at ease with it; the manual says "repeat, then move on").
2. **Voiced continuation:** Wave V (Focus 15) → VI (Focus 21) → VII (23/25/27) → VIII (27+).
3. **The original flavour:** after Wave IV, the Original Series (Exploring, Prospecting) — the unguided exploration experience.
4. **Side branches (any time):** Human Plus functions (after Wave II), Lucid Dreaming (after the Wave I sleep exercise), Chakra and 5th Dimensional (independent), Extra Practices (always).
5. **The early-period series:** alongside Monroe's book, once you know Focus 10.

The app's "Learning path" (Program tab) follows this order with prerequisites; the "Next suggested" card guides you.
