# Maximum benefit — how to get the most out of Gateway

This topic distils the manual's recommendations, the publicly available advice of Institute trainers and decades of user experience into a single "how to practise" guide. The short answer: **regular, rested, with headphones, with a journal and without expectations.** The long answer follows.

## 1. Basic conditions — the non-negotiable five

1. **Stereo headphones.** Hemi-Sync feeds a different frequency to each ear; through speakers or a single earpiece no binaural beat forms. In-ear or over-ear makes no difference; left/right must be worn correctly.
2. **Low volume.** The signal should be "audible but in the background". Lower still for H-Plus. Loud playback distracts and disrupts brain-wave following.
3. **No interruptions.** Phone in flight mode (the app works offline), door closed, household informed. The Institute's CHEC unit is fully dark and silent; at home an eye mask and headphones do the same job.
4. **A rested body.** Excessive fatigue turns the session into sleep. The best hours: on waking in the morning (still near theta) and early afternoon.
5. **Empty stomach, empty bladder.** At least an hour after a meal; no caffeine or alcohol before a session.

## 2. Rhythm — how often, how long

- **Four to six sessions a week**, at most one or two a day. The Gateway Voyage programme does five or six exercises a day, but that is an intensive week-long retreat; what is sustainable at home is one a day.
- **Repeat each session until it feels comfortable** (the manual: "repeat, then move on"). "Comfortable" means: you anticipate the flow of the exercise, the preparatory process comes by itself, and you have experienced a distinct shift of state at least once. Typical repetitions: 2–5.
- **Two to six weeks per wave.** Three to six months for Waves I–IV is normal; whoever hurries returns to "empty" sessions at Focus 15/21.
- **Same hour, same place on weekdays.** The body learns context; identical conditions speed up entry.
- **A break ≠ starting over.** After a break of a week or two, warm up with the previous wave's free flow, then continue.

## 3. Before the session — the 10-minute "descent"

1. Three minutes of Box Breathing or Body Scan (topic 12).
2. Write the intent in one sentence (the journal's opening note). For example: "Scan my shoulder region with the EBT in Focus 12."
3. Choose the session; set the duration (Short/Standard/Long) to the state of the day — Short on a tired day, Long on a good one.
4. Headphones, position, a blanket (the body cools), eye mask.
5. With the first chime, put the phone in the ECB too.

## 4. During the session — the five most common mistakes and their remedies

| Mistake | Sign | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Effort | Frowning, breath-holding, the pressure of "it should" | An attitude of "allowing"; **notice** the body relaxing rather than trying to relax it |
| Analysis | The query "is this Focus 10?" | Put the question in the ECB; leave labelling for the end of the session |
| Sleep | Not remembering the return chime | Move the session to morning; try seated; a small pillow under the head; Stay Awake (H-Plus) |
| Expectation | The disappointment of "nothing happened" | Write the subtle signs in the journal; track the 10-session trend, not the single session |
| Fear | Panicking at vibration or a sense of presence | Stop, feel the REBAL, breathe; recall the protection part of the affirmation; repeat |

## 5. After the session — the three steps that make the gain permanent

1. **Journal (5 minutes, immediately).** Tags + one or two sentences + a drawing if there is one. An experience you don't write down is erased within an hour.
2. **Grounding (3 minutes).** Water, soles of the feet, naming.
3. **A bridge during the day.** Recall the state once during the day with the One-Breath Technique or an H-Plus command. That is how the body learns that the state is "reachable".

## 6. Measuring progress — what to look at

In Gateway, progress is not "I got out / I didn't". Indicators to track:
- **Entry time:** how many minutes to reach Focus 10? (It drops from 15 to 5 minutes over weeks.)
- **State stability:** how long can you stay in Focus 12 without sleeping or thinking?
- **Richness of perception:** the variety of your journal tags (vibration, image, sound, movement, presence, insight…).
- **Carry-over into the day:** in how many seconds can you call up the state with One-Breath/H-Plus?
- **Life effect:** sleep quality, anxiety, focus, creativity — score yourself 1–10 once a month.

The app's Journal tab shows tag frequency and session count; the Program tab tracks progress across the waves.

## 7. Extra rules for the deep levels (Focus 15 and beyond)

- Don't move on to Waves V–VIII before completing at least 40–60 sessions in Waves I–IV.
- Don't follow a Focus 21+ session with another deep session the same day.
- **Never** skip the count on return; if you feel "no time", use the Short duration but complete the return fully.
- Voyager/Union (23–27) can carry emotional intensity (retrievals, losses); practise in periods when you have support, don't push during grief.

## 8. Choosing the audio source

- **Synthesis (default):** the binaural bed generated by the app + spoken guidance; works anywhere; the frequencies are approximations of publicly available descriptions.
- **PC source:** streams the original Hemi-Sync recordings you own from a computer on the same Wi-Fi; Monroe's voice and the original signals. When a match is found the app plays the recording instead of the synthesised bed. If you can listen to the original series, use synthesis only where a recording is missing.
