# Frequently asked questions

Forty questions compiled from the Institute's own FAQs, the manual, user communities and questions specific to this app. Short answers; links to the relevant topic for detail.

## Getting started

**What is Gateway, in brief?** A home-study series developed by Robert Monroe and supported by the audio technology called Hemi-Sync, which teaches you to move between "focus levels" of consciousness. Not meditation but a training in exploration (topic 1).

**I have no meditation experience; can I start?** Yes. Wave I requires no prior knowledge; the preparatory process teaches everything step by step. If you find it hard to relax, warm up with Progressive Relaxation from Extra Practices (topic 12).

**Are headphones really required?** Yes. The binaural beat forms in the brain when each ear receives a different frequency; it does not form through speakers. Any stereo headphones will do (topic 3).

**How soon will I see results?** Most people notice the quiet signs of Focus 10 within 5–15 sessions and the perceptual widening of Focus 12 within 20–40. Dramatic events such as an out-of-body experience can take months or never happen — and the series does not promise them (topic 13).

**What time of day?** On waking in the morning and early afternoon are the most productive. Evening sessions tend to slide into sleep; the night exercises (Exploration Sleep, Mission Night) are designed precisely for that.

**Should I practise every day?** Four to six sessions a week is ideal; at most two a day. Regularity matters more than intensity.

## Experience

**How do I know I'm in Focus 10?** Difficulty feeling the body, not knowing where the limbs are, time shortening, spontaneous images, the sense "the body is asleep but I am here". Don't expect a dramatic event (topic 4).

**What if I fall asleep?** No harm; the app plays the return phase and part of it is processed subconsciously. But learning requires staying awake: morning hours, seated practice, the Short duration (topic 14).

**I don't see anything, is that normal?** Yes. Most perception arrives kinaesthetically (as sensation); visual imagery varies greatly between people. Translate "see" instructions into "feel/know" (topic 14).

**Is the vibration dangerous?** No. The vibrational state Monroe describes is harmless; fear cuts it off. Breathe, feel the REBAL, watch (topic 8).

**I can't get out of body.** Only a small part of the series is aimed at the OBE, and the Institute gives no guarantee. The OBE is not Gateway's purpose but one of its possible outcomes. The road map and methods are in topic 8.

**I felt a guide/presence, is it real?** This guide offers both interpretations: the neurological "sense of presence" and the Monroe tradition's concept of "helpers". The behaviour is the same either way: stay calm, say no to influences that offer less than your intent, write it in the journal (topic 14).

**My dreams have intensified.** Common and usually positive. Keep a Dream Journal; a good foundation for the Lucid Dreaming series (topic 12).

**I cried / old memories surfaced.** Frequent in Release and Recharge and the deep waves; a healthy release. If low mood lasts for days, seek support (topic 16).

## Levels and series

**Can I skip waves?** Not recommended. Focus 15/21 are built on the muscles of Focus 10/12; those who skip return to "empty" sessions. But trying the free flow of the next wave out of curiosity and coming back does no harm.

**The original 1984 series or the 1998 voiced series?** Both are valid. The voiced series makes learning easier; the original (Exploring, Prospecting) gives the taste of unguided exploration. Suggested order: voiced Waves I–IV, then the original (topic 10).

**When do I start Human Plus?** After Wave II. Focus 11 (the Access Channel) is easy to find once you know 10 and 12 (topic 11).

**Is there really no time in Focus 15?** Experientially it feels that way: the perception of time is suspended; sessions feel like minutes. It is a description of a state of consciousness, not a claim about physics (topic 4).

**Is the "Park" of Focus 27 a real place?** In the Monroe tradition it is a "reception station" created by human thought; the Lifeline/Exploration 27 programmes work there. This guide presents it as an experiential map and makes no metaphysical claim (topics 4 and 10).

**Can I progress without attending the Institute's programmes?** Yes; the home series carries the essence of Gateway Voyage, Lifeline and Exploration 27. What the programmes add is the trainer, the group and an uninterrupted week (topic 10).

## Science and safety

**Has Hemi-Sync been scientifically proven?** The perception of binaural beats is well established; the evidence for brain-wave "entrainment" and behavioural effects is mixed and of moderate quality. Gateway's claims about consciousness have not been scientifically tested (topic 3).

**Did the CIA report "prove" Gateway?** No. The 1983 McDonnell report is an army officer's theoretical paper attempting to explain Gateway; it contains no experiments and is not the CIA's institutional view (topics 2 and 3).

**Who should not do this?** With a history of epilepsy, psychosis or bipolar disorder, severe anxiety/dissociation, or a pacemaker, don't start without a physician's approval. In pregnancy, consult a physician about deep sessions (topic 16).

**Can children use it?** The Institute's programmes are for 18+; for the home series it is not recommended beyond parental supervision and short, light sessions (Wave I).

**Can I listen while driving?** No; no Gateway session is to be listened to while driving or doing attention-demanding work. Of the H-Plus commands only the short alertness functions (Stay Awake) may be called.

**Is it addictive?** No physical dependence; but "experience hunting" can be a trap. The journal and the practice of carrying the state into the day keep the balance.

**Does it interact with my medication?** Audio technology has no direct drug interaction; however sedatives and some psychiatric medications increase sleepiness or alter perception during a session. Share it with your physician.

## This app

**Is this app the Monroe Institute's?** No. It is an independent project; not affiliated with, endorsed by or representing the Monroe Institute. Hemi-Sync® and Gateway Experience® are the Institute's registered trademarks. All spoken texts are written originally.

**Does it contain the original recordings?** No. The app synthesises the binaural bed itself. If you own the original recordings, you can stream them from your computer on the same Wi-Fi with PC source mode; for matched sessions the app plays the recording instead of synthesis.

**Are the frequencies the same as the originals?** No; the Institute does not publish its signal design. The app uses approximate values based on public descriptions (Focus 10 ≈ 7.5 Hz … Focus 27 ≈ 0.5 Hz) and says so explicitly (topic 3).

**Does the audio continue when the screen is locked?** Yes; the session plays from a single audio element and media controls appear on the lock screen. Add to the home screen as a PWA; turn off battery saver (topic 14).

**Where are my data?** Only on your device (browser storage). Nothing is sent to a server. Download a JSON backup from the Journal tab; move it to another device with "Restore backup".

**Does it work offline?** Yes, after the first launch.

**How do I change the language?** Settings → Language. Spoken guidance, interface and Guide topics change together.

**Can I turn the visual off?** Yes: with Settings → "Keep screen on" off, the phone locks and the audio continues; or choose the plainest pack (Portal).

**Can I add new techniques or topics?** Yes; the content is JSON/Markdown files (`content/`). The README describes the steps for adding and the validator.

**Can I change the durations?** Every session has a Short/Standard/Long setting; it lengthens or shortens the exploration while keeping preparation and return.

**Will I hear Monroe's voice?** Only in PC source mode, from your own recordings. In synthesis mode the guidance is read by the device's speech synthesiser with original texts.
