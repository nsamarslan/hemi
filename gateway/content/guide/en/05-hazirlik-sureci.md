# The preparatory process — the foundation of every session

The Gateway guidance manual recommends the same preparation before every exercise. This preparation is the single most important step for a session's effectiveness; do not skip it. In the app, every session speaks these steps aloud to you.

## Step by step preparation

[[storyboard:prep]]

### 1. Settling in

Lie down comfortably on your back; a low pillow under your head, your arms falling to your sides. Put on your headphones and keep the volume just loud enough to hear for the whole session. The room should be dim; phone silent, notifications off.

### 2. Relaxation

Close your eyes. Relax every muscle of your body, one by one: feet, calves, knees, belly, hands, arms, shoulders, neck, face, jaw, tongue. Then "feel how you breathe" — let the breath deepen by itself, don't force it.

### 3. The Energy Conversion Box (ECB)

Bring a box before your mind. Put all of today's worries, every question on your mind, the to-do list into this box. Close the lid and set it aside. "This is my space, the box is over there." When the session ends you bring the box back — nothing inside is lost, it is put in order. The box is for **postponing**, not destroying.

### 4. The Gateway Affirmation

Say it within yourself (you don't have to match it word for word; the intent matters):

> "I am more than my physical body. Because I am more than physical matter, I can perceive that which is greater than the physical world. Therefore, I deeply desire to Expand, to Experience; to Know, to Understand; to Control, to Use such greater energies and energy systems as may be beneficial and constructive to me and to those who follow me. Also, I deeply desire the help and cooperation, the assistance, the understanding of those individuals whose wisdom, development, and experience are equal to or greater than my own. I ask their guidance and protection from any influence or any source that might provide me with less than my stated desires."

The manual notes: "It is the intent rather than the words themselves that is important."

### 5. Resonant tuning (the hum)

Take a long breath in through your nose. Part your lips slightly and let the sound come from your throat: "hmmmm" — hum as you breathe out. Feel the vibration: in your throat, your chest, your whole body. Continue for a few breaths like this; then let the breath find its own rhythm. This is the breathing exercise that vitalizes your whole system and reduces inner dialogue.

## The one-breath technique (preparation variant)

[[storyboard:onebreath]]

Right before a session: take a deep breath in through your nose, gather energy from the whole body, carry it up to the crown. Hold the breath, swirl the energy around your brain (for a comfortable length; over time work up to a count of ten). Breathe out with a hum. Instant refreshment.

## Pre-session ritual (from the manual)

- **The same time**: doing it at the same time daily conditions the body to the rhythm.
- **Food**: eat at least an hour before; avoid alcohol, drugs and excessive caffeine — they limit the effectiveness of the signals.
- **Toilet, clothing**: take care of the toilet beforehand; remove tight things like belts, ties, glasses; keep a blanket ready (your metabolism may drop and you may feel cold).
- **Duration and alarm**: set an alarm for long sessions; put the phone in another mode.

## Common mistakes

- Skipping or rushing the preparation — the most common loss of effectiveness.
- Loose headphones or one ear only — the binaural beat breaks.
- Volume too high — the signal disturbs and breaks relaxation.
- Starting hungry or stuffed.
- Phone, watch, noise — the room must be undisturbed.

## Right after the session

- Write in the journal: what did you see, feel, hear? Writing right after the session is the most valuable.
- Ground yourself: feel the soles of your feet, walk barefoot if you can, drink water, eat something.
- Plan the next session; the same time, the same place — repetition of rhythm speeds up learning.
