# Safety, ethics and glossary

This topic has three parts: the safety rules that say who should practise, how and within which limits; the ethical principles of the practice; and a glossary of the terms used throughout the guide.

## A. Safety

### Who should obtain a physician's approval?
- **Epilepsy or a history of seizures.** Rhythmic auditory stimulation can, rarely, affect the seizure threshold; avoid flashing visual packs, practise with the "screen off".
- **A history of psychosis, schizophrenia or bipolar disorder.** Deep relaxation and altered states can trigger symptoms or make interpretation harder.
- **Severe anxiety, panic disorder, dissociative disorder, PTSD.** The sense of losing control, vibrations and "presence" experiences can heighten symptoms. Start with a therapist's support and short sessions.
- **Heart-rhythm disorders, pacemaker, serious blood-pressure problems.** The session is physiologically relaxing and generally safe, but the H-Plus heart/blood-pressure functions do not replace treatment.
- **Pregnancy.** Light sessions (Wave I) are generally fine; consult a physician about breath-retention practices and long deep sessions.
- **Hearing problems, tinnitus.** Low volume is mandatory; stop if the ringing increases.
- **Under 18.** The Institute's programmes are for adults; in the home series, parental supervision and only light sessions.

### Session safety
1. **Never** listen while driving, operating machinery or in water.
2. **Volume low.** For hearing health and signal effectiveness.
3. **Uninterrupted return.** Never skip the return phase; if an alarm or emergency forces you awake, ground for 2–3 minutes afterwards.
4. **Grounding.** At least 3 minutes after deep sessions (15, 21, 23–27) (topic 7).
5. **At most two sessions a day**; one at the deep levels.
6. **Don't combine with alcohol or drugs.** Harmful for both safety and learning.
7. **Night exercises** (Exploration Sleep, Mission Night, Programming Your Dream State) only in bed, when ready to sleep.

### Warning signs — pause the practice and seek support
- Detachment from reality persisting outside sessions, feeling yourself or your surroundings "alien" (derealisation/depersonalisation).
- Panic attacks, recurrent fear-filled sleep paralysis.
- Disrupted sleep, low mood or excessive elation lasting for days.
- Substituting experiences for real-life decisions ("my guide said so").
- Impaired daily functioning, social withdrawal.

### Psychological balances
- **Experience hunting.** Chasing the out-of-body experience or "contact" turns the practice into anxiety. The measure is quality of life, not the dramatic event.
- **Interpretation inflation.** Loading every image with cosmic meaning. Keep the journal, evaluate patterns over time; carry doubt and openness together.
- **Community pressure.** Competition and exaggeration are common in online communities; go at your own pace.
- **Grief and loss.** Voyager/Union (retrieval, transition) stir strong emotions; don't push during acute grief, practise with support.

## B. Ethics

1. **Consent and privacy.** The principle of permission in remote viewing, out-of-body exploration and "healing for another": don't go where you were not invited; don't work on a person without their explicit consent.
2. **The spirit of service.** The essence of the Lifeline/Voyager tradition is balancing your own development with helping others; don't let exploration become a selfish collection.
3. **Honest records.** No exaggeration in the journal; write the "wrong" data too. In remote viewing, score honestly against yourself.
4. **Measure in claims.** When sharing experiences, the language of "this is how it was for me"; don't give others medical, legal or financial direction.
5. **Copyright and trademarks.** Hemi-Sync® and Gateway Experience® are trademarks of the Monroe Institute; the original recordings are copyrighted works. This app uses original texts and distributes no recordings; you stream your own recordings only on your own network.
6. **Sensitivity to commercial and spiritual exploitation.** Stay away from products and people who promise a "certain OBE" or "guaranteed awakening"; Monroe's own attitude was always "find out for yourself, believe no one".
7. **Scientific honesty.** The evidence for Hemi-Sync is mixed; practise knowing and stating this (topic 3).

## C. Glossary

- **Affirmation (Gateway Affirmation):** the statement of intent and protection at the start of every session; begins "I am more than my physical body".
- **AOL (Analytical Overlay):** in CRV, the mind pasting meaning onto raw perception; written aside once noticed.
- **Binaural beat:** the perception of a difference frequency arising in the brainstem when each ear receives a different frequency.
- **Bell / chime:** the sound that marks phase transitions and the return.
- **CHEC unit:** the sound- and light-isolated bed cubicle with headphones at the Institute (Controlled Holistic Environmental Chamber).
- **Compoint:** the reference point fixed in the mind for communication with the total self (Adventure #6).
- **CRV (Coordinate/Controlled Remote Viewing):** Swann–Puthoff's six-stage structured remote-viewing protocol.
- **EBT (Energy Bar Tool):** the imagined bar of energy used in Focus 12 to scan the body and manage energy.
- **ECB (Energy Conversion Box):** the mental box into which concerns are placed before a session.
- **Access Channel:** in Human Plus, the state in which commands are encoded; Focus 11 in the app.
- **FFR (Frequency Following Response):** the brain's tendency to follow an external rhythm; the theoretical basis of Hemi-Sync.
- **Focus level:** Monroe's numerical label for states of consciousness (10, 11, 12, 15, 18, 21, 22–27, 34/35…).
- **Function Command:** in H-Plus, the trigger phrase beginning with "PLUS".
- **Hemi-Sync®:** Hemispheric Synchronization; Monroe's patented binaural audio method and brand.
- **Hypnagogic / hypnopompic:** the states at the threshold of falling asleep / waking; images and the "sense of presence" are common.
- **Ideogram:** the reflex line drawn in response to the coordinate in CRV Stage I.
- **Kinaesthetic perception:** perception arriving as bodily sensation rather than vision (tingling, movement, weight).
- **Retrieval:** in Lifeline/Voyager, helping consciousnesses that have not completed their transition at Focus 23 and accompanying them to Focus 27.
- **Locale I/II/III:** the regions in Monroe's book: the physical world / non-physical systems / parallel realities.
- **Metamusic®:** music albums with embedded Hemi-Sync.
- **Mind Food®:** short skill- or state-oriented Hemi-Sync recordings.
- **MSS (Monroe Sound Science):** the Institute's current audio technology and remastering label.
- **NVC (Non-Verbal Communication):** perception and communication without words; image, symbol, feeling.
- **Null Point:** in Prospecting, the point where the body's electrical polarities neutralise; a window opening in every direction.
- **OBE (Out-of-Body Experience):** the sense of consciousness being located outside the physical body.
- **Patterning:** building the desired outcome in the mind as if already lived; one-month / one-year.
- **Park (Focus 27):** in the Monroe tradition, the reception and orientation area created by human thought.
- **REBAL (Resonant Energy Balloon):** the egg-shaped energy field surrounding the body; a tool of protection and return.
- **Resonant Tuning:** vibrating the body with a humming breath; the last step of preparation.
- **Free Flow:** exploration without direction; the whole of the original 1984 series.
- **Stargate:** the US military's remote-viewing programme, 1970–1995.
- **Carrier frequency:** the base tone on which the binaural beat rides (e.g. 180 Hz).
- **Vibrational state:** the high-frequency vibration/hum felt at the out-of-body threshold.
- **Total Self:** Monroe's name for the person's wider wholeness beyond the physical identity; kin to the "I-There".
- **Grounding:** full return to the body and ordinary wakefulness after a session.
- **WBTB:** Wake-Back-to-Bed; brief wakefulness after 4–6 hours of sleep, then back to bed with intent.
- **Seven resonant colours:** Miranon's sequence of colours mapped to the focus levels (blue, red, yellow, rose, green, purple, white).
