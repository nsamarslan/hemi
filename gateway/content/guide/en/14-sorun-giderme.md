# Troubleshooting — common obstacles and their remedies

Every practitioner hits the same walls; the manual and the Institute's FAQs anticipate most of them. This topic groups the thirty-two most frequent problems by symptom: experiential problems, bodily reactions, practice/rhythm problems and the app's technical hitches.

## A. Experiential problems

**1. "I don't feel anything."**
The most common complaint and usually a misdiagnosis. The manual defines Focus 10 not as a dramatic event but as "body asleep, mind awake" — a quiet state. Look for the signs: difficulty feeling the body, time shift (the session "felt short"), not knowing where your hands are, spontaneous images. Remedy: evaluate the 10-session trend in your journal; write down the "nothing" notes too; put the pressure of expectation in the ECB; train kinaesthetic perception with the Body Scan.

**2. "I always fall asleep."**
Move the session to the morning or early afternoon; try seated; light movement before the session (a 5-minute walk), no caffeine; the Short duration; Stay Awake (H-Plus). If you sleep enough at night the problem largely disappears. Use Discovery's "Exploration Sleep" exercise to turn this weakness into a strength.

**3. "My mind won't quieten."**
A silent mind is not the goal; **not being carried away by thoughts** is. Make the ECB concrete; lengthen resonant tuning (6–8 humming breaths); attach a bodily sensation to each number of the entry count; start with Box Breathing. When a thought comes, say "thought" and return to the count.

**4. "I can't see images."**
Visual imagery varies enormously between people; people with aphantasia (no mental imagery) use Gateway too. Remedy: translate "see" instructions into "feel" or "know"; feel the REBAL as a field on the skin instead of seeing it; build the Body Map as a map of warmth/pulse. Most perception is kinaesthetic.

**5. "The vibration came, I got scared, it stopped."**
Normal. Vibration/hum is the classic threshold of the out-of-body path. The fear reflex fades within the first 3–5 encounters. Remedy: feel the REBAL, breathe; don't try to change the vibration, only watch it; on later attempts "roll it like a wave" (topic 8).

**6. "I get sleep paralysis."**
Harmless and brief. Don't panic; focus on the breath; move one finger, or use the state as a separation gate (the roll-out method). If it recurs often, sleep on your side and look at your sleep schedule.

**7. "I felt a presence."**
The hypnagogic "sense of presence" is a known neurological phenomenon; in the Monroe tradition it is contact with a guide/helper. Whichever interpretation you choose, the behaviour is the same: stay calm, recall the protection part of the affirmation, say "no to influences that offer less than my intent", write it in the journal. If the sense of threat persists, end the session and ground.

**8. "I get lost in Focus 12 / I don't know where I am."**
Stop labelling the state; stay until the chimes call you. Turn on in-session reminders (Settings); the reminder answers the "where am I" question.

**9. "Groggy, dizzy, 'up in the air' on return."**
Insufficient grounding (topic 7). Don't forget to reabsorb the REBAL; complete the count; water, soles of the feet, naming. If it lasts for hours, pause the practice (topic 16).

**10. "Experiences have faded / plateau."**
It happens to every practitioner. Go back one wave; repeat the free flows; lengthen the duration; change the audio source (synthesis ↔ PC); 1–2 weeks of only the preparatory process + Focus 10; take the practice out of bed with the Energy Walk.

**11. "Emotional release, crying."**
Frequent in Release and Recharge, Voyager and Union; usually a healthy letting go. Don't cut the session short, let it flow; afterwards the Loving-Kindness practice and the journal. If low mood lasts for days, seek support.

**12. "Headache / ringing in the ears."**
Volume too high or headphone pressure. Lower the volume; try over-ear instead of in-ear; shorten the session. If the ringing (tinnitus) persists, see an audiologist.

## B. Bodily reactions

**13. Twitches, jerks.** Hypnic jerks; a sign of the Focus 10 threshold. Harmless.
**14. Heaviness/lightness, spinning.** Vestibular recalibration; normal. If unpleasant, open your eyes, a few breaths, continue.
**15. Temperature changes, sweating.** An autonomic shift; use a blanket.
**16. A sense of racing heart.** Usually perceptual; for real palpitations count your pulse (know your resting pulse before the session). If it persists, a physician.
**17. Tingling, "electricity".** The early form of the REBAL and the vibrational state; a positive sign.
**18. Nausea.** Mostly a full stomach or a fast return from deep levels. Slow return, empty stomach.

## C. Practice and rhythm problems

**19. "I can't find the time."** The Short duration + One-Breath; three short sessions a week are worth more than one long one. Move the morning alarm 25 minutes earlier.
**20. "I forgot which session I was on."** The "Next suggested" card in the Program tab; read the journal notes of your previous sessions.
**21. "I can't keep a journal."** Three tags + one sentence is enough; the drawing area helps when words fail. Record a voice note and write it up later.
**22. "I skipped waves and now sessions are empty."** Go back; do the skipped wave's free flow 3–4 times. Focus 15/21 are built on the muscles of Focus 10/12.
**23. "My partner/household is bothered."** Move the session to the morning; short sessions; share the duration and the purpose — mystery breeds discomfort.

## D. The app's technical hitches

**24. No sound / only from one ear.** Is the headphone plug fully in; phone audio routing (Bluetooth may be connected to another device); is the browser tab muted. In-app test: Settings → "Test the binaural sound".
**25. Sound stops when the screen locks.** Add to the home screen as a PWA; turn off battery-saver mode; on iOS check the silent switch; grant the browser background-audio permission. The app shows media controls on the lock screen. On Android the lasting fix is the APK: its session service keeps the audio, the live voice and the bell going with the screen off (item 32).
**26. The PC source won't connect.** Same Wi-Fi; is the server running on the PC (`pc-server.py`); does the firewall allow port 8700; the address is `http://<pc-ip>:8700`. If the app was opened over https, the browser blocks the http PC address — open the app from the address served by the PC.
**27. "No match found" (PC chip grey).** No need to rename the file: tap the chip and choose the right file from the list (the likeliest candidates come first, marked ★). The choice is saved on the PC server, so your other phone or tablet plays the same file. A wrong match is fixed in the same place with "Change" or "Back to automatic matching". To see all your folders, listen to unmatched files or "Assign to session", open the **My library** card on the home page. Added new files? My library → "Rescan".

**28. A long recording started over / two devices have different journals.** For recordings played from the PC, your place (once you have listened for more than a minute) is kept both on the device and on the PC; on start it says "Resuming where you left off", and **Restart** takes you to the top. For the journal, favourites and settings use **Back up to PC** / **Restore from PC** in the journal's backup panel — send from one device, restore on the other; restoring merges and never deletes. These buttons need PC server v0.3 or later.
**29. The visual stutters, the battery drains fast.** Choose a lighter visual pack (Portal) or turn off Settings → "Keep screen on": the screen locks and the audio continues from the lock screen.
**30. The journal disappeared.** Data are kept on the device (IndexedDB); clearing browser data deletes them. Back up regularly (Journal → "Download backup (JSON)"); restore on a new device with "Restore backup".
**31. Won't open offline.** The first launch must be online (service-worker cache); afterwards it works offline. After an update, refresh the page twice.
**32. Android app: no notifications / bell, the session stops in the background.** Settings → Android → "Allow notifications" (Android 13+ shows nothing without asking); if the "alarm permission" row is visible, open it (needed for the exact bell). The persistent notification during a session is normal — it is the service; don't swipe it away; "End" closes the session. If the phone still cuts it, Android settings → Apps → Gateway → Battery → "Unrestricted" (some brands also "auto-start"). The bell rings inside the app and is set as a real alarm too; with the sound off, the alarm channel still vibrates. If an update won't install, the old APK was signed with another key: uninstall and reinstall (back up first: Journal → backup).

## When to stop, when to seek help

- Detachment from reality that persists outside sessions, panic attacks, disrupted sleep, low mood lasting for days → pause the practice, consult a mental-health professional.
- Seizure, fainting, chest pain → urgent medical evaluation.
- A history of epilepsy, psychosis, bipolar disorder or severe anxiety → a physician's approval before starting (topic 16).
