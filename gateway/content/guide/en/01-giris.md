# Introduction — what this guide is, how to use it

This guide is a synthesis of thousands of pages written about Gateway and Hemi-Sync on the internet: guidance manuals, product catalogues, the CIA's 1983 report, academic reviews, the Institute's program pages, archive collections and years of community experience. It does not copy any text verbatim; it brings the information in the sources together, distils it and presents it as readable topics.

## Who is this guide for?

- Those new to the Gateway journey: "where do I start, what are these techniques for?"
- Regular practitioners: those who want maximum benefit from every exercise and a way out when stuck.
- The curious: those who want the map of all series, programs and techniques in one place.

## How to use it together with the app

The guide does not stand alone — it works **alongside** the app:

- **Catalogue tab** — all sessions (145 sessions, 17 waves, 7 series). Tap a session, see its detail, start it.
- **Techniques tab** — 21 techniques from REBAL to Compoint: what each is for, how to do it, when to use it. Each technique has an animated visual preview; you choose the visual pack.
- **Session visual** — choose between six visual packs in Settings (Portal, Energy Flow, The Box, Color Waves, Body Map, Cosmic Images). The chosen pack runs in the background during exercises.
- **PC audio source** — when "Play from the PC" is selected in Settings, your original Hemi-Sync recordings stream from your home PC over the local network; the app size does not change.
- **Guide tab** (this tab) — deep reading. Topic by topic, in two languages.
- **Journal** — after every session: tags, note and drawing. Together with the journaling section in the "maximum benefit" topic, this is the most valuable habit.
