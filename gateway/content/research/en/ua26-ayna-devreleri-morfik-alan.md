# UA-26 — Mirror Circuits, Contagion and Morphic Fields: Where Is the Shared Mind?

This file examines whether a real channel exists between two minds, or whether what we call a "shared mind" is a by-product of a shared code and a shared body. Three rival models are on stage: **(A) the mirror circuit model** — an observing brain motorically imitates the action it sees and thereby "understands" it; **(B) the channelled-sharing model** — the shared mind arises when a signal transmitted through the environment (voice, gaze, gesture, rhythm) couples two brains; **(C) the morphic field model** — sharing is channel-free, a direct "resonance". The verdict is clear: **Model A has collapsed at the level of evidence; Model B is measured and is the most economical; Model C produces no testable prediction and is therefore a metaphor, not a science.** But it does not end there: the collapse of Model A does **not** refute **emotional contagion** — contagion is real, and its carriers are shared signals and shared bodies, not mirrors.

## 2. The Anomaly Inventory (Documented)

### 2.1 The mirror era: the collapse of a brilliant theory

In 1992, neurons in the macaque premotor cortex (F5) were found to fire both when the animal performed an action and when it watched one (di Pellegrino et al.). The theory grew fast: mirror neurons were declared the key to action understanding, imitation, language, empathy, autism — everything.

The counter-evidence chain:

- **Hickok (2009, *Journal of Cognitive Neuroscience* 21:1229–1243) — "Eight problems":** (1) the assumption has **never been directly tested** in monkeys; (2) lesion data show a **double dissociation between action execution and action understanding** (Mahon & Caramazza 2008); (3) monkey mirror neurons **do not respond to pantomimed actions** (Gallese et al. 1996) — so human pantomime tests are, by definition, not probing the monkey-style mirror neuron; (4) "mirror-like" responses also exist in M1 (Tkach et al. 2007), which is consistent merely with **motor preparation**; (5) in humans the **very existence** of mirror neurons is contested (Dinstein 2008; Lingnau et al. 2009).
- **Hobson & Bishop (2017, *Royal Society Open Science* 4:160662):** **mu suppression**, the standard index of the human mirror system, is not a valid mirror index: studies are generally **underpowered**, the mu band is defined differently across labs (8–13, 8–15, 10–14 Hz), and central suppression is **contaminated by attentional and somatosensory** sources. Most EEG evidence for "what mirror neurons do" cannot be carried by its own measure.
- **Heyes & Catmur (2022, *Perspectives on Psychological Science*) — "What Happened to Mirror Neurons?":** the field's current verdict is that mirror responses are **not an innate circuit but a product of sensorimotor learning**. "Mirror" is not the name of a system; it is the signature of learning.

This clarifies the picture: **emotional contagion is real, but not because of "mirrors" — it is explained by learned prediction plus shared signals.**

### 2.2 What is real and solid: contagion and coupling

- **Emotional contagion** has been demonstrated in the laboratory; facial and postural mimicry is automatic (Dimberg 1990: an angry face contracts the corrugator muscle without awareness). Synchronous attraction is measured in speech, gait and posture.
- **Speaker-listener neural coupling:** **Stephens, Silbert & Hasson (2010, *PNAS* 107:14425–14430)** recorded narrator and listener in the same fMRI: the listener's brain **mirrors the speaker's with a delay**, and coupling strength **predicts comprehension**; some listener responses even **precede** the speaker's — the signature of a predictive, common code.
- **Hasson et al. (2012, *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 16:114–121) — "brain-to-brain coupling":** the shared world is built when two brains are coupled **via a signal transmitted through the environment** (voice, gesture, gaze). This is a **channel** model, not a field model — and it is measured.
- **Ritual/community dimension:** swarm rhythm (UA-18) and spatial acoustics (UA-19) are the bodily substrate of this coupling; in groups moving in synchrony the pull is stronger (Wiltermuth & Heath 2009: synchronous walking increases cooperation).

### 2.3 The Sheldrake line: staring and morphic resonance

- **The claim:** in Sheldrake's experiments participants correctly guessed whether they were being stared at in 53–60% of trials (chance 50%); 20 of 21 studies were reported positive; the result was attributed to "morphic resonance".
- **Methodological criticism:** Marks & Colwell (2000) — the trial sequences were **not truly random**: few long runs and many alternations; people can learn such patterns implicitly and exploit them.
- **Direct replication attempt:** **Lobach & Bierman (2004)** ran three attempts: one at chance, two non-significant positive; their conclusion is blunt: *"the staring paradigm is not the easily replicable paradigm that it is claimed to be."* Wiseman's closed-circuit-camera version also reports negative results.
- **Theoretical criticism:** morphic resonance has been found **unfalsifiable** (Shermer 2005) — a frame that can absorb any outcome; its inconsistencies with genetics, embryology and neuroscience have been catalogued. The Jaytee dog case is the clearest illustration of the dead end: the same data were read as positive by Sheldrake and negative by Wiseman — because **no test rule had been written in advance.**
- **The Jung line:** synchronicity narratives are explicable by selective recall and apophenia; apophenia (seeing patterns in randomness) is a documented cognitive tendency. The Jung archive is **valuable cultural material**, not a database.

## 3. Three-Level Excavation

### Level 1 — Observation

What is observed: (a) mimicry and emotional contagion are measured phenomena; (b) speaker-listener neural coupling is measured and predicts comprehension; (c) the mirror-neuron theory rests on untested assumptions and its measure (mu suppression) is criticised; (d) the staring effect has not been consistently obtained under laboratory conditions; (e) morphic resonance generates no falsifiable prediction.

### Level 2 — Pattern

The pattern is this: **the measured part of the shared mind is channel-borne.** The paradigm collapses when the channel is cut: mute the sound, hide the gesture, block the gaze — coupling falls. The morphic model's discriminating prediction would be: **with the channel fully severed, sharing should persist.** That prediction is testable in a clean design — and no laboratory has convincingly shown it.

Second pattern: **contagion's "mirror" name comes from metaphor, not measurement.** The mirror neuron is a beautiful metaphor for understanding; but the same job is done by **predictive coding**: the listener predicts what the speaker will say; when the prediction is right, coupling is high; coupling is not a causal "resonance" but a **delayed reflection of a common code.**

### Level 3 — Why is it like this?

Why did the "mirror" theory spread so fast? Because it served three things at once: a scientific finding (cell recording), an intuition ("to understand is to re-enact"), and a cinematic narrative ("the other's brain imitates yours"). The theory **became popular before it was measured**; HZ-30's epistemic immunity enters exactly here.

Third layer: why does morphic resonance still attract? Because it names something people **experience daily**: long-term partners "sensing" each other. The experience is real; its explanation is more ordinary: a common code, a common history and **very high predictive accuracy.**

## 4. HIDDEN PATTERN CANDIDATE: HZ-26 — Shared-Mind Equation (channel mandatory)

> **Candidate Law (HZ-26).** Sharing between two minds increases with (i) the richness of the common code, (ii) the quality of the physical channel and (iii) the pair's shared history; when the channel is zeroed, sharing falls to chance. The term "morphic field" corresponds to no defined term in the equation.

**Z_sharing ≈ K_commoncode × T_channel × G_history** (K = code richness, T = channel conductance, G = shared history)

**Three predictions:**
1. **Channel severance:** with all communication channels (sound, vision, touch) removed, "synchronous" responses between pairs fall to chance; restoring the channel restores them.
2. **Delay signature:** the channelled model predicts **lag** in listener responses relative to the speaker (and only short-range prediction); a field model predicts **zero-lag** simultaneity. EEG/fMRI data support the lagged + predictive structure (Stephens 2010).
3. **Code does not overwhelm the channel:** under the same channel (e.g. telephone), strangers with low code richness should score low and high-history pairs high — the **code term is measurable independently** of the channel.

**Discriminating test (HZ-26/1 — channel-severance protocol).** A pre-registered three-arm design: (a) full channel; (b) text-only, one-way; (c) no channel (subjects in separate rooms, all signals blocked). Outcome: accuracy at pre-stating the target the partner will see. The field model predicts above-chance accuracy in arm (c); the channel model predicts zero. Baseline: pairs who have never met.

**Second test (HZ-26/2 — pair specificity, random permutation).** Arm (c) data are re-analysed with **permuted partner pairings**: real pairs' scores must be an **extreme value** in the permutation distribution. Prediction: not extreme → "sensing" is a product of chance and prediction, not of the bond.

**Third test (HZ-26/3 — pre-registered re-examination of the staring paradigm).** A design following Lobach & Bierman's warning: truly random sequences (run/alternation distribution tested), observer behind a screen, all cues other than staring (heat, sound, air movement) controlled. Prediction: chance level.

## 5. Evidence Thermometer: 🟢 / 🟡 / 🟠 / 🔴

**Why 🟢?** Mimicry, contagion and synchronous attraction are real, replicated, moderate-sized effects.

**Why 🟡?** Brain-to-brain coupling is measured and predicts comprehension, but causality direction and the "who drives whom" question are still being studied; sub-processes (attention, common stimulus) are not fully disentangled.

**Why 🟠?** The mirror-neuron theory has collapsed among theorists: no direct test in monkeys, contested existence in humans, invalid standard EEG measure (mu). "Collapsed" does not mean "the cells do not exist"; it means "the cells explain everything" has collapsed.

**Why 🔴?** Morphic resonance: no defined carrier, no power law, no falsifiable prediction. It is not a hypothesis; it is a **frame of meaning.**

> **What remains if it is refuted?** If HZ-26/1 produces above-chance sharing in the channel-severed arm, HZ-26 falls and the search for "fields" reopens — the program's most anticipated outcome. On the other hand, the collapse of the mirror theory takes nothing practical away: **empathy training, synchronous work and shared narrative** continue on their own measures; only their explanation shifts from "mirror" to "learning and prediction".

## 6. Practical Implication and Gateway Bridge

1. **The honest promise:** closeness builds a measurable common code; "wordless understanding" in long relationships is real. That is not a supernatural bond but a **high-quality prediction model.** The app should present group sessions in this frame: common rhythm, common breath, common intention — all channel-borne coupling enhancers.
2. **Group synchronisation:** synchronous movement and shared rhythm increase cooperation and trust (Wiltermuth & Heath 2009). This is a solid finding usable without any "collective field" rhetoric.
3. **Warning.** Structures selling courses or packages through "morphic field", "collective unconscious", "telepathic bond" and "signs of synchronicity" are this file's direct subject: these terms **name no measured carrier.** The reality of personal experience is one thing; its explanation requires measurement.
4. **Clinical note:** social isolation and loneliness produce measurable health outcomes (a mortality effect). That is the real need behind the "shared mind"; and its solution is channelled: contact, conversation, shared ritual.
5. **Research note:** this file connects with HZ-03 (receiver-dependent coupling), HZ-36 (interoception) and UA-18 (rhythm flock); in the Φ-2 synthesis the "shared-mind equation" is planned as the **natural experiment that zeroes the channel term** of UA-27 (the mind-to-mind pool): the two files are each other's control arm.
