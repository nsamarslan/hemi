# UA-10 — Is Mythology a Circuit Diagram? The Neuro-Topology of Descent

This file examines the claim that "Sumerian, Egyptian, Greek and Maya descent narratives fit the same four stages one-to-one; mythology is a topological map of the brain's state transitions." The verdict has three parts: (1) **the prevalence and structural similarity of descent narratives are real**; (2) **the "exact four stages" claim carries the classic flaw of selective structuralism** — folklore criticism has found the Campbell line systematically unvalidated; (3) the most productive reading treats myth not as a "circuit diagram" but as **narrative stitches over shared bodily state transitions** (hypnagogia, sleep paralysis, NDE, dissociation). This reading closes the door both to mysticism and to condescension: myths are humanity's oldest maps of its own consciousness transitions — but a map is not a neural wiring diagram.

## 1. Intersection Map: Comparative Mythology, Structuralism, History of Religions and State Neurophysiology All Look at the Same Narrative

Four lines cross here:

1. **Comparative mythology and folklore** — motif indices (Aarne–Thompson–Uther), Propp's functions, the monomyth debate.
2. **Structuralism and history of religions** — Lévi-Strauss on myth structure; Eliade's models of initiation and the "axis mundi".
3. **State neurophysiology** — hypnagogia, sleep paralysis, dissociation, vestibular effects, NDE phenomenology.
4. **Psychedelic neuroscience** — default-mode-network dissolution and the permeabilisation of ego boundaries.

Why do they not merge? Because merging produces a sentence both sides dislike: **myths are not outputs of a shared circuit; they are narratives of shared experiences. The similarity is real, but its source is not a wiring diagram — it is the human body.**

## 2. The Anomaly Inventory (Documented)

### 2.1 Descent narratives: a four-stage skeleton

The following narratives were recorded in cultures independent of one another, with a similar sequence of events:

1. **Inanna's Descent** (Sumer): the queen passes seven gates; at each she surrenders an ornament or garment; she arrives naked and powerless; she is judged, dies, and is later rescued.
2. **Osiris** (Egypt): killed, dismembered, reassembled; resurrection and reintegration.
3. **Orpheus** (Greece): descends to retrieve his beloved; breaks the taboo; loses her on the return.
4. **Popol Vuh** (Maya): the twins descend into Xibalba; endure the death tests; trick death; return transformed.

The common skeleton reduces to four stages: **(i) call and threshold**, **(ii) descent and gates/passages**, **(iii) judgement and death**, **(iv) return and gift (or half-return)**. The claim is that this skeleton is universal not because of random structuralism but because it maps the brain's **state transitions**.

### 2.2 Structural evidence: Propp, Campbell, Eliade

- **Propp (1928)** analysed 196 Russian tales and defined **31 functions**; the sequences were strikingly stable. Propp's model is for the folktale, not the myth — a distinction later often blurred.
- **Campbell (1949)**, in *The Hero with a Thousand Faces*, built the monomyth: **departure – initiation – return**. This schema became the most influential framework of the modern narrative industry (screenwriting, games, film).
- **Eliade** defined initiation as a "death and rebirth" schema; descent narratives are its narrative siblings.

This line shows that the similarity is **real** — but says nothing about its *source*.

### 2.3 Counter-evidence: folklore criticism and the quantitative gap

- **Dundes (2005)** and the folklore tradition are blunt: Campbell mixes genres (myth, folktale, legend — Little Red Riding Hood is not a myth), **selects** examples to fit his theory, drifts away from source texts, and never tests his claim quantitatively. One critical examination reads Campbell's work as "sacred-text rhetoric rather than empirical verification".
- **The quantitative line is limited:** phylogenetic analyses of folktales (Tehrani et al. 2013, the phylogeny of Little Red Riding Hood; d'Huy's myth phylogenies) demonstrate cultural **transmission** and **change** — not a universal skeleton. The Aarne–Thompson–Uther index classifies motif similarities *probabilistically*; it cannot carry a claim as exact as "the same four stages".
- **The diffusion alternative:** for some Near Eastern–Greek–Maya similarities, a shared root (Indo-European transmission) or spread along trade routes is a more economical explanation than independent invention.

### 2.4 The neuro side: the real phenomenology of transition states

Myths may not be circuit diagrams, but people's **state transitions** are real and measured:

- **Hypnagogia:** imagery at the wake–sleep boundary, with threshold and gate phenomena; this boundary is also the stage of UA-02 (hypnagogic download).
- **Sleep paralysis:** waking consciousness + paralysis + pressure/entity sensations; widely discussed as the direct source of the "pressing entity" motif across cultures.
- **NDE:** tunnel, light, life review, "decision to return" features have been measured cross-culturally (Greyson's scales); they overlap strikingly with descent narratives (tunnel = gates; judgement = life review; return = coming back).
- **Psychedelic ego dissolution:** reduced DMN activity alongside ego-boundary permeability (Carhart-Harris et al. 2012) — a voluntary, temporary version of the "death–rebirth" narrative.
- **Vestibular states:** spinning, falling, being drawn downward (linked to UA-32).

So there is a **concrete, measurable shared-source pool** for the common skeleton: the moments when consciousness dissolves and is rebuilt.

## 3. Three-Level Excavation

### Level 1 — Observation

What is observed: (a) descent narratives exist in many cultures and are structurally similar; (b) Propp and Campbell systematised the similarities, but Campbell's work was found selective; (c) quantitative comparisons show motif diffusion, not a universal skeleton; (d) hypnagogia, sleep paralysis, NDE and ego dissolution carry measurable shared phenomenological features; (e) that phenomenology is the raw material for "descent myths".

### Level 2 — Pattern

The pattern is this: **myth is not a circuit diagram; it is a stitch over shared experience.** The human mind encodes its own state transitions in **spatial metaphors**: down/up, gate, river, light/dark, judgement, return. These metaphors arise from shared neural architecture (brainstem arousal systems, limbic valuation, the DMN self-model).

Second pattern: **the "exact four stages" claim may be an artefact of narrative richness.** Rich narratives fit any rich skeleton a little; the real test is whether the **order** of stages and their mutual dependence beat randomised models — and that test is mostly not done.

### Level 3 — Why is it like this?

Why did almost every culture produce a "descent" narrative? Because **every human periodically experiences consciousness dissolving and being rebuilt** — sleep, illness, fever, birth, trauma, hypnagogia, grief, ageing. These transitions share a common feeling: "something was taken from me, and then given back."

Third layer: **myth is the social binder of these transitions.** Initiation rites turn the inner experience into a public performance: descent, judgement, death, return — so that the person lives the experience once **under control**. That is why descent narratives never die: each generation tells its own transition with the same skeleton.

## 4. HIDDEN PATTERN CANDIDATE: HZ-10 — Shared-Source Motif (neuro-phenomenological stitching)

> **Candidate Law (HZ-10, revised).** The similarity of descent narratives arises not from a shared "neural circuit diagram" but from shared **state-transition phenomenology**: consciousness dissolves → threshold/gate experience → judgement/encounter → reconstruction. Motif fidelity is bounded by the fidelity of the underlying experience; cultural variation concentrates in the **stitching idiom** (gods, places, symbols), not in the core sequence.

Simplified expression:

**M_motif ≈ Σ (F_experience × D_stitch)**, where F are phenomenological transition constants (dissolution, threshold, judgement, return) and D are cultural narrative stitches.

**Three predictions:**
1. **Order specificity:** across qualified narrative corpora, the four stages must appear in **this order** significantly more often than under random permutations; the test must measure "ordering", not mere "presence".
2. **Phenomenological marker overlap:** descent narratives must over-represent the markers found in NDE/hypnagogia reports (tunnel/passage, gate, water, judgement, light, "return") independently of culture.
3. **Independent state test:** independent of narrative, the same four-stage order should appear in hypnagogia and NDE reports more often than under randomised transition models.

**Discriminating test (HZ-10/1 — blind structural coding, pre-registered).** A stratified narrative sample (Sumerian, Egyptian, Greek, Maya + control narratives from independent cultures) is coded blind for stages; inter-coder reliability is measured; schema fit is compared against a **shuffle baseline**. Prediction: order is significant — but the effect size comes out at the level of a **tendency**, not "one-to-one".

**Second test (HZ-10/2 — phenomenological overlap).** 500+ NDE and 500+ hypnagogia reports are coded with a pre-registered lexicon and compared with the marker distribution of descent narratives. Prediction: "passage" and "return/commissioned task" markers are over-represented in both pools.

**Third test (HZ-10/3 — independent ecosystem).** The same skeleton is searched in phenomena with no contact with the narrative tradition (e.g. modern sleep-paralysis reports, intensive-care delirium accounts); prediction: the core order appears there too — because the source is not culture, it is the body.

## 5. Evidence Thermometer: 🟢 / 🟡 / 🟠 / 🔴

**Why 🟢 (phenomenology)?** Hypnagogia, sleep paralysis, NDE and ego dissolution are documented with scales and laboratory conditions, and independently replicated.

**Why 🟡 (motif prevalence)?** Prevalence is real but dependent on classification systems and coding choices; it is a statistical pattern, not an exact match.

**Why 🟠 (the "four stages" claim)?** The Campbell line has been found selective; independent quantitative validation is limited; the diffusion alternative can explain many similarities.

**Why 🔴 ("myth = circuit diagram")?** The claim that myth is a neural circuit diagram produces no measurable mapping; the neuro-phenomenological shared-source model, by contrast, is **testable** — so that model is the real program.

> **What remains if it is refuted?** If order specificity fails in HZ-10/1, the "universal skeleton" claim concedes that folklore criticism was right, and what remains is the **phenomenological shared-source pool** (hypnagogia, NDE, dissociation) and its clinical value. In the other direction: if order specificity + phenomenological overlap co-occur, mythology becomes usable as a serious **data source** for neuro-phenomenology — one of the strongest bridges in the Φ-2 synthesis.

## 6. Practical Implication and Gateway Bridge

1. **The honest promise:** myths are humanity's oldest maps of consciousness transitions; reading them can help a person make sense of their own threshold experiences (sleep boundary, grief, illness, transformation). But this map is **pedagogical**, not a neural circuit diagram.
2. **Application bridge:** the "descent / level transitions" language of the Gateway narrative should be read, on this file's frame, as a **state-transition pedagogy**; it must carry no mechanism claim.
3. **Clinical value:** structured frameworks of NDE and hypnagogia phenomenology can be used in post-traumatic meaning-making and sleep-boundary work; their effectiveness is a separate clinical research question.
4. **Warning.** Selling the "descent myths are neural circuit diagrams" frame together with a personal-growth or health promise is misleading. Ritual and myth reading produce meaning; **treatment claims must rest on evidence.**
