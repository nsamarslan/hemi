# UA-03 — The Zoonoetic Field: The Biosphere's Own Internet

This dossier examines the claim that "the biosphere works like a brainless internet: trees talk through fungal networks, flocks decide as a single mind, butterflies that find their way generations later are coupled to the field." The conclusion is threefold: (1) **the physics of coordinated collective behaviour and animal navigation is real and impressive** — but the mechanisms are local and require no "global broadcast"; (2) **mycorrhizal networks exist** — but the narrative that "trees talk and send warnings to seedlings" has been seriously weakened by the literature; (3) **a single "biosphere internet" field is unsupported**: biological signalling consists of thousands of private protocols, each locked to its receiver.

## 1. Intersection Map: Mycorrhizal Ecology, Flock Physics, Insect Navigation and Quantum Biology Stare at the Same Question

Four lines intersect here:

1. **Mycorrhizal ecology** — common mycorrhizal networks (CMNs), carbon transfer, the "wood-wide web" narrative.
2. **Collective behaviour physics** — starling murmurations, fish schools, criticality and scale-free correlations.
3. **Insect navigation** — the multi-generation migration of the monarch butterfly, sun compass and circadian clock.
4. **Quantum biology / magnetoreception** — the cryptochrome radical pair and the magnetic compass.

Why do they not converge? Because converging produces a sentence that unsettles both sides: **biological signalling is real, but "internet" is a broadcast metaphor; the actual system is a market of signals running on local rules, each locked to its receiver.** There is no mind-like "field"; the field is what local protocols *look like* from outside.

## 2. Anomaly Inventory (With Slugs)

### 2.1 Mycorrhizal networks: existence proven, the "internet" claim collapsed

Mycorrhizal fungi really do connect tree roots; the **physical existence** of common mycorrhizal networks is well documented. But the popular "wood-wide web" narrative was shaken by a systematic critique in 2023: **Karst, Jones and Hoeksema (2023, *Nature Ecology & Evolution* 7:501–511)** examined three popular claims:

1. **"CMNs are widespread in forests"** — insufficient evidence; too few forests mapped.
2. **"Seedlings receive resources and benefit through CMNs"** — in a review of 26 studies, seedlings generally did not benefit; evidence of benefit and harm was roughly equal, and the most common outcome was **neutral**.
3. **"Old trees send resources or insect-damage warnings to seedlings"** — there is **not a single peer-reviewed field study** supporting this claim.

The authors also documented **positive citation bias**: once an optimistic result is published, later papers cite it as stronger than it was, building a chain of misinformation.

In fairness, the other side is recorded too: **mycoheterotrophic plants** (chlorophyll-less plants feeding from fungal networks) are presented as natural evidence of resource movement through CMNs (*Nature Plants*, 2024); another reply argues the carbon-transfer evidence is "solid and accumulating" (PMC10751480). So the correct verdict: **the network exists and carbon flows — but the flow is small, its direction and CMN-causation are contested, and the language of "communication" has outrun the evidence.**

### 2.2 Flock physics: criticality without a central controller

Starling murmurations turn like a single body without a visible leader or global control. **Cavagna et al. (2010, *PNAS* 107:11865–11870)** showed that velocity correlations in murmurations are **scale-free**: correlation length grows with flock size — the system sits near a critical point where the smallest local piece of information can reach the largest scale. **Ballerini et al. (2008, *PNAS* 105:1232–1237)** found that interaction range is *topological, not metric*: each bird interacts with roughly **six or seven neighbours**, regardless of distance.

This is the dossier's strongest 🟢 piece: **globally looking behaviour emerges from local rules.** No broadcast, no receiver-transmitter layer, is needed.

### 2.3 The monarch butterfly: a genetic compass, not a network

Monarchs reach their Mexican overwintering grounds across four generations. The mechanism is solved: the sun compass is calibrated by a **circadian clock in the antennae** — **Merlin, Gegear and Reppert (2009, *Science* 325:1700–1702)** showed that clock-shifted antennae shift the butterfly's orientation. So "finding the target generations later" is not field coupling; it is a **genetically encoded compass + clock**.

### 2.4 Magnetoreception: solid behaviour, contested mechanism

The behavioural evidence for the avian magnetic compass is strong. The molecular candidate is the **cryptochrome-4 radical-pair** mechanism (Ritz et al. 2000; Xu et al., 2021, *Nature* — cryptochrome-4 dependence in migratory birds; review: Hore & Mouritsen 2016, *Annual Review of Biophysics*). Two cautions: (1) the mechanism (spin coherence in radical pairs) remains debated; (2) the language of "entanglement-coupled fields" is pop physics — radical-pair chemistry measures *coherence*, not a *biological entanglement network*.

### 2.5 Other real signalling layers

- **Quorum sensing** (Bassler and colleagues): bacteria switch collective behaviour according to cell density — a genuine mechanism that looks like population-level memory and decision-making.
- **Plant volatile signals and priming**: Karban and colleagues; volatile warnings can be copied between neighbouring plants — but this is an *airborne local* signal, not a network.
- **Fungal electrical activity**: Adamatzky (2022) recorded electrical "spikes" in mycelium; the functional interpretation that they carry information is **not established**.

## 3. Three-Level Excavation

### Level 1 — Observation

What is observed: (a) mycorrhizal networks physically exist and carbon isotopes are detected moving through them; (b) the claim that "seedlings benefit from the network" was **not supported** in the systematic review; (c) the "warning signal" claim has **no** peer-reviewed field evidence; (d) flock scale-free criticality and topological interaction have been measured; (e) the monarch's navigation mechanism is solved as clock + sun compass; (f) magnetic compass behaviour is strong, the molecular mechanism contested; (g) quorum sensing is real.

### Level 2 — Pattern

The pattern is this: **there is no "internet" in the biosphere; there are "signal markets."** Every biological signal is bound by three hard conditions: (1) **physical access** (diffusion, hyphal network, airflow — limited range), (2) **receiver matching** (a receptor; no match, no signal), (3) **energetic cost** (broadcasting is expensive; an organism invests only when a receiver exists). So the system looks like a network from outside — from inside, it is thousands of private protocols.

A second pattern: **mindless-looking intelligence emerges from criticality.** A flock is not a "shared mind"; it is the property of a critical system to carry the smallest local difference to the global scale. That is not design; it is *physics*.

### Level 3 — Why is it so?

Why can the biosphere not build a single network? Because "broadcast to everyone" is economically meaningless in nature: no organism pays for a signal with no receiver. Selection strengthens **channels that have receivers**; channels without receivers decay. So the biosphere is a mosaic of local protocols without a global layer.

Third layer: **the "field" feeling is a product of scale.** Millions of local couplings, superimposed, generate cross-scale correlations; a mind looking from outside (ours) reads them as a single "consciousness." The zoonoetic field is probably **not an illusion but a sum**: channels, none of which is an "internet," inevitably adding up to something that looks like one.

## 4. HIDDEN PATTERN CANDIDATE: HZ-03 — Receiver-Locked Coupling (the Field Without a DMN)

> **Candidate Law (HZ-03, corrected).** Biological signalling does not run on a global field but on the product **local rule × receiver matching**. Total behaviour looks like an "internet" because millions of local couplings generate cross-scale correlations — but the system has no central broadcast, no shared address space, and no conscious field.

Simplified statement:

**K_effect ≈ Σ_i (Y_i × A_i)**, where Y is the strength of the local rule and A the receiver match; distance/range limits break the effect exponentially. **∂(effect)/∂(global broadcast) ≈ 0**

**Three predictions:**
1. **The receiver decides:** the magnitude of CMN-mediated resource transfer should vary systematically with receiver species and genotype; no "everyone receives" pattern should appear.
2. **Criticality scales with scale:** in flocks, correlation length should grow with group size, but no global oscillatory mode should appear; a small local perturbation should spread like an avalanche (the Cavagna line).
3. **Navigation is individual:** monarch/bird orientation should be explained without any within-population "field knowledge" — each individual's own clock + compass; cutting the individual off from clock/compass breaks orientation (Merlin 2009), cutting it off from the community does not.

**Discriminating test (HZ-03/1 — mycorrhiza, preregistered and blind).** In field plots with three tree species, radioisotope-labelled carbon + molecular genotyping (who is connected to whom) measure transfer; the claim is that transfer magnitudes will be **small and receiver-dependent**, and seedling performance will be predicted by light and nutrients, not by network composition.

**Second test (HZ-03/2 — flock perturbation).** A single bird/fish in a flock is artificially stimulated; prediction: the effect spreads in a way predicted by criticality scaling laws — and its speed/shape is explained by local interaction topology, not "field coupling."

**Third test (HZ-03/3 — fungal electrical function).** Mycelial electrical activity is examined with blind experiments designed to test the functional claim (is there a stimulus-locked, repeatable signature?). Prediction: spiking is explained physiologically (nutrient/oxygen flows); the "communication" claim requires further evidence.

## 5. Evidence Thermometer: 🟢 / 🟡 / 🟠 / 🔴

**Why 🟢?** Flock physics (criticality, topological interaction), quorum sensing and the monarch compass mechanism are independent, replicated findings.

**Why 🟡?** CMN existence and carbon transfer are real; but the importance, direction and causality of the function remain contested.

**Why 🟠?** "Mother trees share resources" and "warning signals" claims: the systematic review did not support seedling benefit; the warning claim has no peer-reviewed field evidence.

**Why 🔴?** The "conscious biosphere field" claim: no measurement, no defined mechanism, no testable prediction.

> **What would remain if falsified?** If HZ-03/1 finds strong, receiver-independent, large-scale transfer, the "receiver-locked coupling" sentence weakens and CMNs rise to a genuine distributed resource system. In the opposite direction: if transfer is shown to be small and receiver-dependent, what remains is **scale-free criticality, quorum sensing and compass mechanisms** — already standing independently and needing no "internet" metaphor.

## 6. Practical Takeaway and Gateway Bridge

1. **The honest promise:** the sense of "coupling" with nature is not romantic but real — however, the channel of that coupling is not a field but **receiver-locked local protocols**. This does not shrink the experience; knowing which gate you pass through makes the practice sound.
2. **Forest practice:** silent walking produces real, measurable effects through volatile signals and attention dynamics; "mentally talking with a tree" is an unverifiable frame.
3. **Flock/collective practices:** the expected effect in group rituals and synchrony work is not "field coupling" but criticality and synchrony produced by local interaction rules (joins UA-18).
4. **Caution.** This dossier does not belittle any "nature-awareness" practice; but it prescribes no "coupling with the forest" as a treatment for joint pain, allergy or medical conditions. Clinical matters belong to physicians.
5. **Research note:** this dossier builds the "distributed systems" family with UA-22 (magnetic sense), UA-04 (extracerebral distributed memory) and MP-4; the Φ-2 synthesis will test the "field" metaphor against HZ-24 sleep synchronization.
