### Horizon Excavation — 40-Area Hidden-Pattern Program (UA-01 – UA-40)

Forty independent frontier areas processed in order of evidence strength. Each area puts disconnected disciplines on one table, inventories the anomalies with full citations, performs a three-level excavation, and closes with a Candidate Hidden Pattern stamped by an Evidence Thermometer. Thermometer: green = independent laboratory evidence, amber = meaningful data with ongoing debate, orange = preliminary data or strong theory, red = failed replication. The goal is a fifth excavation layer above the biophysical and historical-flow layers, neither bowing to consensus nor accepting any claim unexamined.
