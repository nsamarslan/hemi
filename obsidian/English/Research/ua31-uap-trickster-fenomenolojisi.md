# UA-31 — Liminality and the Trickster: Why Does Anomaly Hide Itself?

“It vanished as soon as I turned on the camera” can mean two things: an event genuinely difficult to record exists; **or** narratives are selected while footage is absent, and well-recorded cases acquire ordinary explanations. The folkloric *trickster* (boundary-crossing, deceptive figure) is a compelling metaphor for phenomena both seen and elusive. But a metaphor is not a mechanism. This dossier reconstructs the loss-of-record pattern across UAP (unidentified anomalous phenomena), twentieth-century sighting "waves," witness memory and government reports as a **measurable selection model**. **Unidentified does not mean supernatural; explained does not mean the witness lied.**

## 1. Intersection Map: Folklore, Flight Safety, Intelligence Archives and Memory

Four specialists do different work on the same report. A **folklorist** traces a motif across cultures: fairies/spirits, airships, flying saucers and today's UAPs. A **pilot and sensor engineer** ask about range, optical geometry, IR sensor calibration and parallax. An **archivist** asks what criterion kept a file: why does a folder contain only the “unresolved”? A **psychologist** considers attention, source memory, social conversation and changes in account over time. None can decide ontology from a single angle.

“Trickster” can be a valuable **historical/interpretive metaphor**; its testable version asks: do events systematically impede documentation, or do we retain events in anomaly collections *because* we could not document them? The former position must quantitatively exclude the latter.

## 2. Anomaly Inventory: Real Records, Missing Detail

### 2.1 Historical flow: motifs and controls evolve together

Jung's *Flying Saucers: A Modern Myth* (1958) reads flying saucers as a psychic image of its era; this is an interpretation of mind, not a theorem that no physical event occurred. Jacques Vallée's *Passport to Magonia* (1969) juxtaposes recurring fairy and alien accounts; motif similarity is **not evidence for a single actor**. Project Blue Book collected U.S. reports from 1947 to 1969; files that remained unidentified later are not “unseen” but **not distinguishable with their surviving records**. The public military clips after 2017, NASA's 2023 methods report and the AARO series represent new institutional gates. Because *which events receive an official report?* changes at every stage, a raw “wave” graph measures both the sky and changes in reporting channels.

### 2.2 Institutional data: scope and limits

AARO's FY2024 report lists **757 new or retrospectively filed reports**; **118** cases were resolved during the reporting period as ordinary objects (balloons, birds, unmanned aircraft), and a further **174** queued for review were ultimately classified as prosaic. These denominators refer to different decision stages; they cannot be added casually to claim “all reports resolved.” Others still need data; AARO states it found no evidence of extraterrestrial beings or technology. Its FY2025 report records **319** new reports, **114** resolved and **205** outstanding; it also resolved **256** older cases. Do not interpret 114/319 as “the fraction of identifiable physical objects”: outstanding is a class of **unfinished case files**, not a certified new category of physical object.

NASA's September 2023 independent study stresses the scarcity of high-quality records and the possibility that stigma suppresses reporting; it is a **roadmap for better collection**, not a catalogue pronouncing judgments on each historical case. AARO has institutional archive access, but not every classified datum is public in the text; neither “the state disclosed everything” nor “missing data prove concealment” follows.

### 2.3 Sensor geometry and human narratives

An angular speed in one camera cannot establish **physical speed without distance**: a fast-moving spot may be a nearby insect, a remote object, camera rotation or parallax. The IR image's color palette reflects **device processing** as well as heat. Starlink flares, balloons, clouds, aircraft and optical scatter are documented counterexamples; resolving one case does not automatically explain the next. A good record pairs raw sensor stream, focus/zoom, time, direction, altitude and weather.

A witness need not be “deceptive.” Expectation, poor visibility, group discussion, delayed recall and question wording convert a physical stimulus into a *story*. Rich, consistent accounts fit both a common real stimulus and a shared cultural code. Independence is not established by “three people said the same thing” but by **three first reports sealed separately before mutual discussion**.

### 2.4 How can “vanishing on demand” arise?

Imagine that of 100 well-documented cases, 90 are solved as balloon/satellite/bird and only 10 stay in the “unknown” folder; of 100 poorly documented cases, 15 are solved and 85 remain. **Those are illustrative thought-experiment counts, not actual UAP statistics.** Conditioning on open files raises the share of poor data to 85/95. “Anomaly escapes evidence” can thereby emerge from **record selection**. The reverse-causation question: as sensor quality improves, does the rate of genuinely *anomalous physical signatures* decline, or only the rate of “open cases”?

## 3. Three-Level Excavation

**Level 1 — Observation:** genuinely unresolved reports exist; agencies have linked many to ordinary objects; sparse data dominate archives. The intensity of a witness's experience and the report's physical origin are separate matters.

**Level 2 — Hidden pattern:** two selection filters stack up. The first is **reporting**: surprising, narratable events are selected, while stigma silences others. The second is **resolution**: well-documented ordinary events close quickly; poorly documented cases stay open. Sampling only open files is biased toward both surprise and uncertainty.

**Level 3 — Why does it recur historically?** Technology changes the sky; culture changes its symbols; the logic of missing data changes less. The 1950s saucer and the 2020s sensor pixel are different carriers of the same “I cannot identify this.” The recurrence therefore proves **neither extraterrestrial agency nor universal hallucination**. No specialist sees flight records, motif histories and selection mathematics through a single case; their combination puts the trickster in its proper place: first a **data-pipeline property**, and only then, perhaps, a physical question.

## 4. HIDDEN PATTERN CANDIDATE: HZ-31 — Liminal-State Steering

> **Candidate law:** Within anomaly collections, documentation and explanation are **inversely selected**: as record quality grows, ordinary cases leave the folder faster; low-quality survivors become more open to interpretation. If that selection predicts the appearance of “hiding,” no additional intelligent concealer is required.

`P(open file | high evidence quality) < P(open file | low evidence quality)` is the ordinary ordering, but this inequality **does not prove the underlying event was ordinary**. A second mapping is `A_perceived ≈ U_uncertainty × M_motif × S_selection`. These are unmeasured parameters, a map for designing experiments, not measured real-world coefficients.

**Discriminating experiment /1 (prospective recording):** sequentially enroll *all* events in a region before resolution or media attention. An independent team labels time, calibration, weather, flight/satellite tracks and each witness's first report. Prespecify the “high-quality” threshold (two independent calibrated cameras plus reliable distance/time); analyze every event with the same blinded protocol. The primary endpoint is the rate of unexplained *physical performance* **by record quality**, not just the share assigned “open.” A documentation-resistance hypothesis must predict that as quality improves, not only the unknown label but a prespecified anomalous signature declines or documentation systematically fails; without a declared mechanism it is outside the test.

**Experiment /2 (narratives):** obtain separate anonymous statements in the first 30 minutes; repeat for the same witnesses before and after social-media exposure. Coders are blind to the alleged wave. Measure **growth of motif alignment with exposure** and stability of the perceptual core; a null laboratory exercise does not establish an extraterrestrial ontology.

**Experiment /3 (negative control):** run the same pipeline on known satellite flares, meteors, drones and false alarms; if the “trickster” pattern appears for known stimuli, it lacks specificity to the object's origin. If an anomaly persists under full controls, publish it for independent scrutiny rather than dismiss it in advance.

## 5. Evidence Thermometer and Falsification Box

**🟢** The existence of UAP reports, gaps in device/documentation quality and documented ordinary resolutions. **🟡** Jung/Vallée motif parallels are interesting as narrative analysis but face selected examples and cross-era coding problems. **🟠** HZ-31's selection hypothesis is testable; prospective calibrated evidence of a special “anomaly evades documentation” process is absent. **🔴** Building an ontology from stories as proof of an intentional trickster actor or transpersonal consciousness is unsupported.

> **If falsified, what remains?** If a prospective two-sensor study finds well-documented, repeatable events resistant to ordinary explanations, the selection model **cannot suffice on its own**: new physics, unknown technology or other concrete candidates must compete openly. Conversely, if “hiding” dissolves as cases become known, **the reality of the witnesses' experiences and the flight-safety imperative** remain; unknown does not turn into “lie.” A cross-cultural motif does not itself yield a common agent.

---

## 6. Practical Implications and the Gateway Bridge

An entity impression, inner voice or time distortion at sleep onset is a real subjective event, not evidence of an external being. The app encourages a record, safety and clinical help if needed; it should not design an “elusiveness” narrative as a proven power. In flight observations, trained witnesses should be heard for safety, their identities protected and raw data/seal times preserved. HZ-26's “shared code” can explain discussion between witnesses without denying a stimulus. HZ-30 requires the **denominator behind the open case**: unexplained does not mean inexplicable.
