# Human Plus (H-Plus®) — function exercises and command encoding

Human Plus is a "practical life" series Monroe published between 1987 and 1989, separate from Gateway. Gateway teaches you to explore consciousness; H-Plus teaches you to **use the explored state instantly in daily life**. The idea is simple: in a special Hemi-Sync-supported state (the Access Channel) you encode a "function"; later, during the day, without headphones, you call that function with nothing but a short command word. This topic covers the system, the 53 functions and the rules of correct use.

## 1. How the system works

Every H-Plus tape had two sides:

- **Side A — the Preparatory Exercise:** relaxation, resonant tuning, the H-Plus affirmation and entry into the **Access Channel**. In this app the Access Channel is modelled as Focus 11: between Focus 10 and 12, a state where the body is asleep and the mind awake, yet open to "inner programming". Monroe describes it as "the channel through which you reach your total self".
- **Side B — the Function Exercise:** in the Access Channel, the function of that tape (for instance "relax") is experienced through the senses and paired with the **Function Command**. The command always begins with the key word **PLUS**: "Plus Relax", "Plus Recall", "Plus Let Go".

**Daily use:** At the moment of need, say the command to yourself (or whisper it). If the encoding is strong, the body-mind pattern switches on by itself. The booklet: "results are subtle; wait at least two minutes; some functions settle in after three or more sessions."

## 2. Rules of use (from the 1989 booklet)

1. **Volume very low.** H-Plus signals were designed for low volume; loud playback neutralises them.
2. **Preparatory Exercise first.** If you are new, listen to the Preparatory Exercise; do the Function Exercise **at least one day later**.
3. **One function a day**; you may repeat the same function on several days.
4. **Patience.** "Nothing happened" the first time is normal; the command strengthens with repetition.
5. **Combine.** Functions support one another: Relax + Let Go, Attention + Recall, Sleep + Restorative Sleep.
6. **Medical functions do not replace treatment.** Heart, Hypertension, Emergency Injury and the like are supportive; share them with your physician.
7. **While driving** call only short alertness functions (Stay Awake, Think Fast); never the deep ones (Relax, Sleep).

## 4. Anatomy of a command

A command has two or three parts: **PLUS** (the key) + the function word (+ reinforcement). The repetition in "Plus Heart, Better, Better" is a reinforcement of rhythm and intent. The booklet's rule: the command is paired with the **sensation** you experience at the moment of encoding; the word itself is not magic. That is why "really feeling the relaxation" during the Function Exercise matters more than merely repeating the command.

**What to do at the moment of encoding**
1. Enter the Access Channel; in H-Plus the request for "protection" is given by the affirmation rather than the REBAL.
2. **Live** the function: for Relax feel every muscle melting, for Recall feel the information you seek lighting up, for Light Foot feel the body becoming lighter.
3. At the peak of the experience say the command to yourself three times; "glue" the sensation to the word.
4. Stay quietly for a few minutes; return.

## 5. How H-Plus relates to Gateway

- Gateway tools (REBAL, EBT, patterning) are inherently long-form; H-Plus turns them into **instant** triggers. Examples: Release and Recharge (Gateway) ↔ Let Go + Recharge (H-Plus); One Month Patterning ↔ Möbius West; Problem Solving ↔ Options + Wake/Know.
- Starting after Wave II is ideal: experience of Focus 10/12 makes Focus 11 easy to find.
- H-Plus's language of "protection" and "total self" is the same as the Gateway affirmation; the two systems do not conflict.

## 6. Realistic expectations

H-Plus carries ambitious names (Immunizing, Regenerate). Even the 1989 booklet says "effects vary from person to person and are subtle and cumulative". Controlled research is almost non-existent; the strongest evidence is user reports. This app presents H-Plus as a **practice of self-regulation and intent**: in behavioural functions such as relaxation, attention, sleep and habit change, user experience is consistent; for physiological claims (immunity, blood pressure) consult your physician and never interrupt treatment.

## How to use this in the app

- Catalogue → Human Plus series → a function session (about 20–30 min). A verified command appears as a chip at the top of the card.
- First time: the Preparatory Exercise (the session at the start of the series) first, the first function the next day.
- **Command of the day** (home card): one of the functions you have encoded comes forward each day in turn; say its command silently whenever you need it and tap **+1** on every use. The counter (today / 7 days / total / day streak) also shows on the session page and in the Programme tab; "Another command" changes the day's pick.
- For a function whose command is not verified from public sources the card says "Note the command": listen to the original recording and note it yourself; the note stays only on your device and in your backup.
- Settings → **Command reminder**: while the app is open, reminds you of the command of the day every 1–4 hours (as a notification if you allow it; real alarms come with the Android package).
- Write the effect in your journal in short notes — after 3–4 weeks you will see which functions have "settled".
- Session visual: plain "Portal" or screen off; H-Plus needs no visual focus.
