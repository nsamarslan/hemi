# History — the story of a consciousness research from 1958 to 2026

## Origins (1956–1971)

Robert A. Monroe was an American radio broadcaster and entrepreneur. In 1956, his company's research and development division was working on sleep learning. While experimenting on himself, Monroe noticed that his mind could go outside his body while his body slept. In 1958 he recorded his first controlled out-of-body experience (OBE). These experiences took him beyond psychoanalysis, hypnosis and the psychology of his time.

Two things happened in 1971: the landmark book **Journeys Out of the Body** was published, and an institute was founded in Faber, Virginia — first as the Mind Research Institute, later as **The Monroe Institute**. The term "out-of-body experience" entered the public vocabulary with this book.

## The birth of Hemi-Sync (1970s)

Throughout the 1970s, Monroe systematised the finding that brain hemispheres could be synchronised by giving each ear a slightly different frequency. He named the technology **Hemi-Sync** (hemispheric synchronization). In 1975 the Institute was granted a **US patent** (US 3,884,218) for using such sound pulses to induce frequency following response.

In the same period the Institute applied hemispheric synchronization to relaxation, stress reduction, pain control, accelerated learning, concentration, creativity and sleep. Monroe's cautious and honest note — "Hemi-Sync signals can easily be rejected if they are not in agreement with your conscious or unconscious desires" — summarises his approach.

## The birth of the Gateway (1973–1983)

According to the guidance manual, it all began in 1973 at Whistlefield Research Laboratories in Afton, Virginia. The Esalen Institute (Big Sur, California) heard of Monroe's work and invited him for a weekend workshop. The demand was so great that the workshops were repeated; the program was first called the **M-5000 Program**. As the process developed, it was renamed the **Gateway Voyage** program: a "Gateway" — a means by which the individual moves into the exploration, knowledge and understanding of oneself — and of "the many reality systems where one has existence".

From 1977 the Gateway Voyage became the Institute's six-day residential flagship program. Its home-study adaptation became the **Gateway Experience**: six albums (waves), six exercises each — thirty-six cassettes in total: **Discovery, Threshold, Freedom, Adventure, Exploring, Prospecting**.

## The CIA report and Stargate (1983)

On 9 June 1983, Lieutenant Colonel **Wayne M. McDonnell** of the US Army INSCOM wrote a report titled "Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process". The report examined the mechanism of the Gateway process (binaural beats, hemispheric synchronization, the ECB, resonant tuning, the REBAL, the EBT, remote viewing) and was released to the public by the CIA in 2003 (page 25 is still withheld). It records Monroe's note of an OBE tape at "beta ~2877.3 CPS" (about 2877 Hz) and the Schumann resonance (~7–7.5 Hz). At the time the US military was running the **Stargate** remote viewing program; the Gateway was assessed in that context.

## The original series and reissues (1984–1996)

The Gateway Experience cassette series was released from 1984. **Wave V Exploring** and **Wave VI Prospecting** came out in 1984 as fully instrumental (voice-free) free-flow exercises. Cassette sets followed in 1989–1992 and CD reissues in 1996. Waves I–IV carry the same exercise names in the original and later voiced series.

## Human Plus (1987–1989)

In 1987 Monroe and Fred "Skip" Atwater announced the **Human Plus (H-Plus®)** program: function exercises designed to use the power of the subconscious to create change in physical, emotional and mental functions — each teaching a short verbal command. The 1989 booklet lists 49 functions (sleep, attention, circulation, habit, memory, senses...). The command is encoded while the Access Channel (Focus 11) is open; you call on it any time in daily life.

## Waves of Change — the voiced series (1998–2004)

From 1998 the series was re-recorded with voice: **Waves of Change**. Waves I–IV (Discovery, Threshold, Freedom, Adventure) were re-produced with the same exercise names, voiced by A.J. Honeycutt. **Wave V Exploring** (1998) became the voiced introduction to Focus 15, replacing the old free-flow Exploring. **Wave VI Odyssey** (2004, voiced by Laurie Monroe) provided the first at-home access to Focus 21, replacing the old Prospecting.

## Lucid Dreaming and other album series (1999+)

In 1999 the **Lucid Dreaming** series (4 exercises: Mind Awake/Body Asleep, Creative Imagination, Flying Solo, Programming Your Dream State) was released. Dozens of Hemi-Sync album series followed: the Chakra Attunement collection (Chakra Meditation, Chakra Journey, Heal and Balance), the 5th Dimensional Transformation collection (Morning Exercise, Releasing Early Morning Anxiety, Transformation, Exploring Our Future...), the Mediumship series, and series for sleep, learning, concentration and the Metamusic albums.

## The Miranon and Explorer archives (1974–2023)

During the Institute's **Explorer** research sessions in 1974–1990, a being called "Miranon" is reported to have conveyed information about the Focus levels and the "seven resonant colors" (through an explorer named Leona with Shay Ellsworth, later drawn by Nancy McMoneagle; compiled by Daniel Erickson in 2023). These session notes were published as a digital archive in 2020–2023. Topic 4 of this guide explains the system.

## Recent years (2019–2026)

- **2019 — Wave VII Voyager**: Focus 23/25/27 (the transition areas, belief system territories, the reception center), based on the Lifeline program.
- **2022 — Wave VIII Union**: beyond Focus 27; 4 CDs, 8 exercises; based on the Exploration 27 program.
- **2023 — Gateway Voyage Online**: the online form of the flagship program; 23 guided sessions, 26 hours, with Monroe's original voice and Monroe Sound Science (MSS) remasters.
- Today the Institute runs residential programs (Gateway Voyage, Lifeline, Guidelines, Exploration 27, Starlines, Heartline, Timeline, EM², OBE Spectrum, Lucid Dreaming Intensive, Going Home) and online courses; the Hemi-Sync catalogue reaches dozens of series.
