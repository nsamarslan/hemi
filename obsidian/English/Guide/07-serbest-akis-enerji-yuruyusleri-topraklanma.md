# Free flow, energy walks, grounding — unguided exploration and the way back

Gateway's first exercises guide you; the later ones gradually **fall silent**. That silence is deliberate: the goal is to learn to trust the flow of your own consciousness. This topic covers the three legs of unguided exploration: free flow, awareness in motion (energy walks) and the complement of every exploration — the return (grounding).

## 1. Free flow — not "doing nothing" but "feeling everything"
![[sb-freeflow-1.en.svg|360]]
1. After preparation and entry, hold an intention, or hold none at all.

![[sb-freeflow-2.en.svg|360]]
2. Let the mind drift; whatever comes, observe it.

![[sb-freeflow-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Do not judge or interpret; just notice.

![[sb-freeflow-4.en.svg|360]]
4. Stay there until the bells call you back; then return slowly.
**What is it?** The manual's "Free Flow Methods of Exploration" section describes exploration without direction: set a goal (or don't), let go into the flow, meet whatever comes. The series is rich in free-flow exercises: Free Flow 10 (Discovery #6), Free Flow 12 (Adventure #3), Free Exploration in Focus 15 (Exploring #6), Free Flow Journey in Focus 21 (Odyssey #6), Free Flow 27. **Every exercise of the original 1984 series is free flow** — no spoken narration, only the Hemi-Sync signals.

**Three forms of free flow**
1. **Goal-less:** Hold no intent; simply stay and notice. Let the mind be a "blank screen" — everything that arrives is data.
2. **With intent:** Hold a one-sentence intent ("contact with my guide", "the answer to this question", "what my body needs") and let go. The intent is the compass; the flow is the wind.
3. **Mission:** The approach of the Exploring series — go out in Focus 10 or 12 with a specific mission (Mission 10/12/Day/Night). The mission is the nail that keeps the mind awake.

**How to do it**
- Complete the preparation and entry phases fully (REBAL included).
- State your intent (if any) once; then **do not repeat it**.
- Observe whatever comes without judging: image, feeling, sound, movement, nothing.
- When the feeling "nothing is happening" arrives, observe that feeling itself; the deepest shift is usually right behind this moment.
- Stay until the chimes call you; don't cut the time short yourself.

**What to expect in free flow** The manual's "Gateway Entry" section says perception may be kinaesthetic: sensations of rising and sinking, swaying, sliding, tilting; "electric" sensations — tingling, vibration; twitches, pulses, pressure, temperature change. Ask not "has my foot relaxed?" but "how does my foot feel?" These sensations come and go; they form patterns.

**Your first free flows may feel empty.** That is the nature of the practice. Emptiness is consciousness waiting to be told "what shall I do?"; in time it stops waiting and starts flowing.

**Journal:** Free flow stays half-finished without a journal. Write immediately after every session: even the vaguest sensation gains meaning in later sessions.

## 3. Energy walks — awareness in motion
![[sb-walks-1.en.svg|360]]
1. Enter Focus 12: with a session or on your own, eyes open.

![[sb-walks-2.en.svg|360]]
2. Start walking; feel awareness in the soles of the feet at every step.

![[sb-walks-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Open up to the surroundings: trees, sky, ground, wind.

![[sb-walks-4.en.svg|360]]
4. Gather energy from the surroundings with each step; store it at the centre of the body.
**What is it?** The manual's "Energy Walks" section: moving from place to place in Focus 12 awareness — on a real walk or mentally. The aim is to keep awareness while moving and to gather energy from the surroundings.

**How to do it (a real walk)**
1. Before setting off, stand still for 2–3 minutes; deepen the breath; do a short resonant tuning (hum).
2. Start walking: feel the soles of your feet at every step; let your steps blend with the rhythm of the ground.
3. Open to the surroundings: trees, sky, wind, birdsong, sun. Each is a source of energy.
4. Draw energy from the surroundings as you breathe in; release fatigue as you breathe out.
5. Passing a tree, reach a hand toward it (touching is not required); feel an "exchange".
6. At the end of the walk, stop; store the gathered energy at the centre of your body.

**Mental energy walk:** Lying down, in Focus 12, walk a favourite nature path in your mind — with the same steps. The app's "Energy Walk" session (Extra Practices) takes this form.

**Why it matters** Most people practise Gateway only lying down; the energy walk is the first bridge for **carrying the state into daily life**. Walking barefoot multiplies the energy exchange.

## 4. Grounding ("Need Grounding?") — the complement of every exploration
![[sb-grounding-1.en.svg|360]]
1. Get up slowly; stay seated or standing for a while.

![[sb-grounding-2.en.svg|360]]
2. Feel the soles of the feet; draw the earth's energy up like roots.

![[sb-grounding-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Drink water, eat something; remember the body.

![[sb-grounding-4.en.svg|360]]
4. Walk barefoot; run your hands over soil and grass.

![[sb-grounding-5.en.svg|360]]
5. Light movement: stretch, swing the arms, drop the shoulders.
**What is it?** The manual's "Need Grounding?" section explains what to do when you can't fully return to the body after a session. Signs: slight dizziness, grogginess, feeling "up in the air", detachment from the body, excessive looseness, difficulty concentrating, sometimes mild nausea.

**Why does it happen?** At deep levels (especially 15/21 and beyond) consciousness moves away from the body; if the return phase is rushed or the REBAL is not taken back, part of you stays "out there".

**How to ground (manual + common practice)**
1. **Get up slowly.** Sit first, take a few breaths; then stand.
2. **Soles of the feet.** Press your feet into the floor; draw the earth's energy up through the soles; imagine a root.
3. **Drink water, eat something.** The body returns to itself through "digestion"; a salty or heavy bite works fast.
4. **Touch.** Rub your hands on earth, grass or a tree; walk barefoot if you can.
5. **Move.** Stretch, shake your arms, drop your shoulders; a few squats.
6. **Name things.** Count five objects around you aloud; say today's date — cognitive "anchoring".
7. **Cold water.** Wash your face; hold your wrists under cold water.

**In the app:** Extra Practices → the short "Grounding" session; every session's return phase already ends with "bring your energy back into your body". After deep sessions, make 2–3 minutes of extra grounding a habit.

**When to see a doctor?** If dizziness or grogginess lasts for hours, or there is panic or a feeling of detachment from reality, pause the practice and consult a physician (topic 16).

## 5. The return ritual — from free flow back to daily life

Every exploration is completed by its return. A good return ritual:
1. Chimes → count from your current level to ten, from ten to waking.
2. Absorb the REBAL; bring your energy into the body.
3. Move your fingers and toes; open your eyelids.
4. Write in the journal (tags: vibration, image, sound, movement, presence, emotion, insight, body feeling, time shift, deep silence, dreamlike).
5. Ground (steps above).
6. Plan the next session; same time, same place.

- Reminders (Settings → In-session reminders) can be "off" or sparse during free flow; this is the setting closest to the original mood.
- Night free flows (Mission Night, Exploration Sleep) end with a dark screen and no chimes; write in the journal in the morning.
