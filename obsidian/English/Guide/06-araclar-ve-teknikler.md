# Tools and techniques — Gateway's mental toolkit

Gateway is not a "listening programme"; it is a **toolkit**. Each wave teaches a new tool, and the later waves apply that tool at deeper levels. This topic gathers every tool from the Guidance Manual in one place: what it does, how it is done, which exercise teaches it and how the tools combine. Each technique has an in-app counterpart in the Techniques tab, complete with an animated preview.

## Map of the tools — which wave teaches what

- **Wave I Discovery (Focus 10):** the preparatory process, the Energy Conversion Box, the Gateway Affirmation, resonant tuning, entry into Focus 10, **REBAL** (Advanced Focus 10), release and recharge, sleep exploration, free flow.
- **Wave II Threshold (Focus 12):** entry into Focus 12, problem solving, **One Month Patterning**, **Color Breathing**, the **Energy Bar Tool (EBT)**, the **Living Body Map**.
- **Wave III Freedom (10/12):** Liftoff, **Remote Viewing**, **Vectors**, **Five Questions**, **Energy Food**, **First Stage Separation**.
- **Wave IV Adventure (12):** **One Year Patterning**, Five Messages, Free Flow 12, **NVC I–II**, **Compoint**.
- **Waves V–VIII:** the tools are now applied at new levels (15, 21, 23–27); new territories arrive instead of new tools.

## 1. REBAL — the Resonant Energy Balloon
![[sb-rebal-1.en.svg|360]]
1. Once in Focus 10, imagine an egg surrounding your body.

![[sb-rebal-2.en.svg|360]]
2. Feel the energy inside the egg: a tingling, a field, a faint shell.

![[sb-rebal-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Send the energy out of the crown, around the body, back in through the feet; grow the balloon with every loop.

![[sb-rebal-4.en.svg|360]]
4. Throughout the session the balloon surrounds and protects you; nothing from outside gets in.

![[sb-rebal-5.en.svg|360]]
5. On return, fold the balloon inward and absorb it; let the energy come back into the body.
**What is it?** In Focus 10 you build an egg-shaped balloon of energy around your body. The manual describes it as "your energy body": it envelops you, protects you and brings you safely back wherever you go. It is the seat belt of out-of-body work.

**How to build it**
1. Move into Focus 10; deepen your breathing.
2. Feel energy fountain out of the top of your head, flow down around your body, meet beneath your feet and rise again — a continuous circulating flow.
3. Let the flow wrap your body in an egg: head at the top, feet at the bottom, an arm's length in every direction.
4. Feel the inside of the balloon: tingling, warmth, slight pressure or a sense of "field". The type of sensation is personal.
5. Expand or tighten the balloon as you wish; remember its presence throughout the session.
6. On return, fold the balloon inwards and **absorb** the energy back into your body. This is the concrete step of coming home.

**When?** Learned in Advanced Focus 10; built every time you separate, run a mission, free-flow or go deep. In the Guidelines programme the balloon also becomes the vehicle that carries you to Focus 15 and beyond ("float the balloon upward into Focus 12, then 15; lower it back to Focus 10 on return").

**Tips:** The balloon is a field, not a wall — you can move inside it. Never skip the absorption step on return; the most common cause of a "floaty" afterglow is a balloon that was never taken back.

## 2. The Energy Conversion Box (ECB)
![[sb-ecb-1.en.svg|360]]
1. Eyes closed; see a box in front of your mind.

![[sb-ecb-2.en.svg|360]]
2. Put everything circling in your head (tasks, the meeting, the bill, the phone) into the box one by one.

![[sb-ecb-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Close the lid. Set the box aside: 'This is my space; the box is over there.'

![[sb-ecb-4.en.svg|360]]
4. After the session bring the box back; review what is inside and take back what you need.
**What is it?** A container you bring before your mind and fill with everything that weighs on you today. The manual calls it a "mental container": its purpose is to keep every distraction "outside" for the duration of the session.

**How?** See the box → place worries, tasks and questions in it one by one → close the lid → set it aside → at the end of the session take it back and review the contents.

**Creative variants:** the box can be a chest, a safe, a well, a planet, even a cube of light. The app's "Box (ECB)" visual pack is a glowing cube with rays flowing into it — you can imagine your concerns turning into light and streaming into the box. The shape is unimportant; the sense of **being able to let go** is what matters.

**Tip:** Put "energy" in the box too: drop the day's fatigue in with it. The box is for postponing, not destroying.

## 3. The Gateway Affirmation
![[sb-affirmation-1.en.svg|360]]
1. In preparation, after the box: body lying still, mind quiet. The affirmation starts here.

![[sb-affirmation-2.en.svg|360]]
2. 'I am more than my physical body.' Feel awareness reaching past the body's edges; expand, experience, know.

![[sb-affirmation-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Second part: ask for help, guidance and protection from those with greater knowledge and experience.
The session's statement of intent; the full text is in topic 5. It has two parts: (1) "I am more than my physical body… I deeply desire to expand, to experience, to know, to understand, to control, to use" those energies; (2) a request for help from beings wiser than oneself and for protection from influences that would offer less than one's stated desires. The manual: "the intent matters, not the words."

## 4. Resonant Tuning (humming)
![[sb-tuning-1.en.svg|360]]
1. Breathe in long and deep through the nose; let the chest fill.

![[sb-tuning-2.en.svg|360]]
2. Part the lips slightly; let the sound come from the throat: 'hmmm'. Hum the breath out.

![[sb-tuning-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Feel the vibration: in the throat first, then the chest, then the whole body.

![[sb-tuning-4.en.svg|360]]
4. Continue for a few breaths; then let the breath settle on its own.
Deep in-breath through the nose, out-breath on a hum ("hmmm"), vibration felt in throat, chest and body. It energises the whole system and quietens inner chatter. It is the last step of preparation and, on its own, a stress reliever during the day.

## 5. The Energy Bar Tool (EBT)
![[sb-ebt-1.en.svg|360]]
1. In Focus 12, imagine holding a bar of light in both hands.

![[sb-ebt-2.en.svg|360]]
2. Pass the bar over the body: feet, legs, belly, chest, arms, head.

![[sb-ebt-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Stop where you feel a knot, coldness or heaviness: gather it, carry it off, balance.

![[sb-ebt-4.en.svg|360]]
4. After scanning the whole body, lower the bar; rub your hands together.
**What is it?** The first active tool taught in Focus 12. You imagine a bar of energy held at both ends; you scan your body with it, clear knotted or murky places, and gather, move and balance energy.

**How?**
1. In Focus 12 feel a bar in your hands; you decide its length, weight and colour.
2. Pass the bar slowly over your body: feet → legs → abdomen → chest → arms → head.
3. Stop wherever you feel a knot, coldness or heaviness: "gather this, move it, balance it."
4. You can "charge" the bar: draw energy from sun or earth into it, then pass it into the body.
5. When done, lower the bar; rub your hands together.

**When?** In every body session from Threshold onward; together with the body map in Bio-Body (Prospecting #2); and in healing work directed at others (at a distance, by intent).

**Tip:** A quick scan clears the surface, a slow scan clears the depths. The bar works like a vacuum cleaner: it draws, gathers and carries.

## 6. The Living Body Map
![[sb-bodymap-1.en.svg|360]]
1. In Focus 12 feel the living map inside the body: skeleton, organs, pulsing points.

![[sb-bodymap-2.en.svg|360]]
2. Give your attention to one region: the liver, the lungs or the hands, for example.

![[sb-bodymap-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Watch whatever you feel there: warmth, coolness, pulse, flow.

![[sb-bodymap-4.en.svg|360]]
4. If needed, support that region with the Energy Bar Tool.
**What is it?** Visualising your body's inner structure in your mind: skeleton, organs, vessels, energy pathways. The more alive the map, the more precisely your EBT scan hits its target.

**How?** In Focus 12 feel a "map" inside the body → direct attention to one region (liver, lungs, hands) → observe the sensation there (warm, cold, pulse, flow) → support it with the EBT. No medical anatomy is required; it is an intuitive map. Wherever you can feel your pulse is a "node".

## 7. Color Breathing
![[sb-color-1.en.svg|360]]
1. Breathe a colour in: red, for example. Let it enter through the nose and fill the chest.

![[sb-color-2.en.svg|360]]
2. Direct the colour to where the body needs it: the liver, the lungs, the hands.

![[sb-color-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Breathing out, release the grime, tiredness and tension like grey smoke.

![[sb-color-4.en.svg|360]]
4. Go through the seven colours in turn: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet.
**What is it?** Adding the seven basic colours (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet; finally white) to the breath and spreading them through the body. Each colour passes like a wave of cleansing. The manual presents it alongside its "Brain Power" section.

**How?** Breathe a colour "in" → send it to the region that needs it → breathe the residue out in that colour → work through all the colours in turn. If you cannot see the colours, **feel** them; feeling comes before image.

**Link:** It is a cousin of Miranon's seven resonant colours (topic 4); the app's "Color Waves" visual pack is built on this theme.

## 8. Patterning — one month, one year
![[sb-patterning-1.en.svg|360]]
1. In Focus 12, state the outcome you want in one clear sentence.

![[sb-patterning-2.en.svg|360]]
2. See that outcome as if it were happening now: where, with whom, how it feels.

![[sb-patterning-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Add feeling to the picture: joy, gratitude, pride. Feeling is the cement of the pattern.

![[sb-patterning-4.en.svg|360]]
4. Set the pattern in place: 'This is my new state.' Say it and step out.
**What is it?** Building the desired outcome in the mind as if it had already happened. The manual: "creating the future from today"; the mind processes the lived and the not-yet-lived the same way. Two exercises: One Month Patterning (Threshold #3) and One Year Patterning (Adventure #1).

**How?**
1. State the goal in one sentence ("one month from now …").
2. Build the moment as if you were living it: where, with whom, what you feel.
3. **Add emotion**: joy, gratitude, pride. Emotion is the cement of a pattern.
4. Settle the pattern into place: "this is my new state" — then leave it; don't keep fiddling with it.
5. Do a short "pattern refresh" every morning (combines well with Human Plus Möbius West).

**Mistake:** hoping is not patterning; patterning is feeling the lived result. A blurry goal yields a scattered result.

## 9. Energy Food
![[sb-food-1.en.svg|360]]
1. In Focus 12, feel the field around your body.

![[sb-food-2.en.svg|360]]
2. Choose a source: the sun, a tree, the earth or the sea.

![[sb-food-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Draw a stream of energy from that source: breathe it in.

![[sb-food-4.en.svg|360]]
4. Store the energy at the centre of the body; spread it from there through the whole body.
**What is it?** Topping up the body's hunger for energy from the environment: earth, sun, tree, air, water. Freedom #5 in the manual.

**How?** In Focus 12 feel the field around your body → choose a source → draw a stream from that source, breathe it in → store it at your centre and spread it through the body. If a real tree, sea or sun is nearby, the source grows stronger. Energy is like food: take as much as you need.

## 10. Release and Recharge

**What is it?** Discovery #4. Noticing and letting go of old fears and negative emotions, then filling the vacated space with fresh energy. It is Gateway's "emotional cleansing" exercise.

**How?** In Focus 10 call up a fear or feeling → see it as an object → "this no longer serves me" → let it go (into the box, the wind, the light) → fill the space with REBAL energy. Start with small fears; the big ones come in time.

## 11. Problem Solving (Focus 12)

Threshold #2. Carry a problem into Focus 12 without "searching" for the solution; ask the question, let it go, and allow the answer to arrive as image, feeling or word. Powerful when combined with morning patterning. The answer usually arrives hours after the session, at an unexpected moment — which is why you keep a journal.

## 12. Vectors

Freedom #3. The "aiming" tool of remote viewing: you direct your perception toward a target like a vector — in space ("north, a hundred metres"), in time ("yesterday morning"), even toward a person. It is kin to the coordinate logic of CRV (topic 9).

## 13. Five Questions
![[sb-fiveq-1.en.svg|360]]
1. In Focus 12, ask your question silently.

![[sb-fiveq-2.en.svg|360]]
2. Wait for the answer: an image, a feeling, a word, or silence.

![[sb-fiveq-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Note the answer; do not doubt it, just record it.

![[sb-fiveq-4.en.svg|360]]
4. Ask follow-up questions if needed: each answer opens the next question.
Freedom #4. Questions put to the total self (your wider, more knowing self); the answers arrive through non-verbal communication. Ask → wait → note → proceed by keeping records rather than by doubting; the journal tests the accuracy of the answers over time.

## 14. Five Messages

Adventure #2. Five messages from the total self: unasked-for, often symbolic communications. Don't rush to interpret; write the message down and its meaning unfolds over the following days.

## 15. NVC — Non-Verbal Communication I and II
![[sb-nvc-1.en.svg|360]]
1. In Focus 12 open the mind: ask a question or hold an intention.

![[sb-nvc-2.en.svg|360]]
2. While waiting for the answer, do not search for words; let images, colours and feelings come.

![[sb-nvc-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Note everything that comes: symbol, colour, feeling, place.

![[sb-nvc-4.en.svg|360]]
4. In time you learn your own symbol language: the same image, the same meaning.
**What is it?** Perceiving and communicating without words: images, symbols, feelings. The manual calls it "the language of intuitive thought". Adventure #4–5.

**How?** In Focus 12 hold a question or intent → don't look for words; let image, colour and feeling come → note everything that arrives (symbol, colour, feeling, place) → over time your own symbol dictionary forms. Seeing the same symbol again means the symbol is "tightening".

## 16. Compoint — the Communication Point
![[sb-compoint-1.en.svg|360]]
1. In Focus 12, have your first contact through NVC: a presence, an image, a feeling.

![[sb-compoint-2.en.svg|360]]
2. Fix that point of contact in your mind: a place, a colour, a feeling.

![[sb-compoint-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Visit the same point every session; the contact grows stronger each time.

![[sb-compoint-4.en.svg|360]]
4. From there ask questions and receive messages; the exchange is non-verbal.
**What is it?** A reference point you fix in your mind where you make contact with the total self; it is built on top of NVC. The manual: "it is like your telephone number." Adventure #6.

**How?** Have your first contact through NVC → fix that point (a place, a colour, a feeling) → visit it every session → ask and receive messages from there. The contact point is personal; don't lose its location.

## 17. Remote Viewing and Liftoff
![[sb-rv-1.en.svg|360]]
1. In Focus 12 extend your perception beyond the body's boundaries.

![[sb-rv-2.en.svg|360]]
2. Hold a target: coordinates, a place or an event.

![[sb-rv-3.en.svg|360]]
3. Direct perception at the target; record the first impressions: colour, texture, feeling. Do not interpret or name.

![[sb-rv-4.en.svg|360]]
4. Move stage by stage as detail grows (ideogram, senses, dimensions…); write everything in the journal.
Freedom #1 Liftoff teaches the sensation of "taking off" from Focus 10 into 12; Freedom #2 Remote Viewing teaches perception beyond the body. Details in topic 9.

## Combining the tools — the chains the manual suggests

- **Body work:** Body Map → EBT scan → Color Breathing → Energy Food.
- **Emotional cleansing:** ECB → Release and Recharge → refill with REBAL.
- **Decision / insight:** Problem Solving → Five Questions → NVC → visit Compoint → journal.
- **Goal setting:** Patterning (one month) → morning refresh → Möbius West (H-Plus) → one-year pattern.
- **Out-of-body preparation:** REBAL → Liftoff → separation methods (topic 8) → absorb REBAL → grounding.
